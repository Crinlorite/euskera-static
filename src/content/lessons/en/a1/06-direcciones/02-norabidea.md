When someone asks you how to get somewhere — or when you get lost and have to ask — you need a handful of imperative verbs and adverbs of direction. In this lesson, we'll cover the essentials.

## Basic Imperatives

In Basque, the affirmative imperative of movement verbs is very direct:

| Form | Base verb | Meaning |
|---|---|---|
| **Jo** | jo | hit / head (jo ezkerrera = turn left) |
| **Segi** / **Jarraitu** | segitu / jarraitu | follow, continue |
| **Hartu** | hartu | take (hartu kale hau = take this street) |
| **Igo** | igo | go up |
| **Jaitsi** | jaitsi | go down |
| **Sartu** | sartu | enter |
| **Irten** | irten | exit |
| **Zeharkatu** | zeharkatu | cross |
| **Iritsi** / **heldu** | iritsi / heldu | arrive (… arte = "until…") |

> *Jo* literally means "to hit," but it's used metaphorically as "to head towards": **jo ezkerrera** = "turn left."

## Adverbs and Directional References

| Basque | English |
|---|---|
| *ezkerra* | the left |
| *eskuina* | the right |
| *aurrera* | forward |
| *atzera* | backward |
| *zuzen* | straight |
| *gora* | upward |
| *behera* | downward |

> Note: to indicate **direction towards**, the suffix `-ra` is used (which you'll see in depth in lesson 03): *ezkerre-ra* (to the left), *aurre-ra* (forward), *atze-ra* (backward). The same goes for *gora* and *behera*.

## Typical Landmarks

| Basque | English |
|---|---|
| *semaforoa* | traffic light |
| *bidegurutzea* | crossing, intersection |
| *zebrabidea* | crosswalk |
| *biribilgunea* / *errotonda* | roundabout |
| *aldapa* | hill, slope |
| *eskailerak* | stairs |
| *zubia* | bridge |
| *errepidea* | road |
| *kalea* | street |

## Example Phrases

- **Jo ezkerrera bidegurutzean.** — Turn left at the crossing.
- **Segi aurrera semaforora arte.** — Go straight until the traffic light.
- **Zeharkatu zebrabidea.** — Cross the crosswalk.
- **Igo eskailerak eta sartu eraikinean.** — Go up the stairs and enter the building.
- **Jaitsi aldapa, eta gero jo eskuinera.** — Go down the hill, and then turn right.
- **Egin atzera; gehiegi joan zara.** — Go back; you've gone too far.

## Asking for and Giving Directions

> — *Barkatu, **non dago** udaletxea?* — Excuse me, where is the town hall?
>
> — ***Segi aurrera** semaforora arte. Han **jo eskuinera** eta **zeharkatu** plaza. Udaletxea elizaren ondoan dago.* — Go straight until the traffic light. There, turn right and cross the square. The town hall is next to the church.
>
> — *Eta gertu **dago**?* — And is it nearby?
>
> — *Bai, oso gertu. Bost minutu oinez.* — Yes, very close. A five-minute walk.

> **Tip:** many directions follow a fixed pattern: imperative (*jo*, *segi*, *zeharkatu*) + place with a suffix (*ezkerrera*, *semaforora*, *zebrabidea*). If you memorize the pattern, you can chain long instructions by combining pieces.
