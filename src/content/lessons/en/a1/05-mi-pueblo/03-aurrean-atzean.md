Once you know how to say "it's in X", the next step is to say **exactly where**: on, under, next to, behind… In Basque, these positions are always constructed with the pattern **reference point + position**.

## The basic positions

| Basque | English |
|---|---|
| *aurrean* | in front |
| *atzean* | behind |
| *ondoan* | next to |
| *gainean* | on, on top of |
| *azpian* | under |
| *artean* | between |
| *ezkerrean* | to the left |
| *eskuinean* | to the right |
| *barruan* | inside |
| *kanpoan* | outside |
| *erdian* | in the middle, in the center |

## How to build them: -(r)en + position

The basic formula is **(reference)-(r)en + position**. The reference takes the genitive `-(r)en` (which is like "of the" in English):

| Basque | English (literal) |
|---|---|
| **mahaiaren gainean** | on the table (lit. "of the table, on") |
| **etxearen aurrean** | in front of the house |
| **elizaren atzean** | behind the church |
| **parkearen ondoan** | next to the park |
| **kutxaren barruan** | inside the box |
| **bi mendien artean** | between two mountains |

Pattern: the reference comes first, **then** the position. It's the exact opposite of Spanish.

## Complete examples

- **Liburua mahaiaren gainean dago.** — The book is on the table.
- **Katua aulkiaren azpian dago.** — The cat is under the chair.
- **Eliza plazaren erdian dago.** — The church is in the center of the square.
- **Botika eskolaren ondoan dago.** — The pharmacy is next to the school.
- **Ni Maiteren eta Anaren artean nago.** — I am between Maite and Ana.

## Adverbs of place: hemen / hor / han

To point things out quickly, there are three adverbs — and watch out, there are **three distances**, not two like in Spanish:

| Basque | English | When |
|---|---|---|
| *hemen* | here | near the speaker |
| *hor* | there | near the listener |
| *han* | over there | far from both |

> These three adverbs correspond to the demonstratives *hau / hori / hura* and their long locative forms *honetan / horretan / hartan* ("in this / in that / in that one over there").

Quick examples:

- **Hemen** taberna bat dago. — There is a bar here.
- **Hor** parkea dago. — The park is there.
- **Han** dago euskaltegia. — The euskaltegi is over there.

## Mini-conversation: playing hide-and-seek

> — *Pilota mahaiaren azpian dago?* — Is the ball under the table?
>
> — *Ez, pilota ez dago mahaiaren azpian.* — No, the ball is not under the table.
>
> — *Aulkiaren atzean dago?* — Is it behind the chair?
>
> — *Bai, hor dago!* — Yes, there it is!

> **Tip:** all these positions already include the `-an` suffix (*aurr-ean*, *atz-ean*, *azp-ian*…). You don't need to add anything else.
