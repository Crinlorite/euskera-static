A good part of social life in Euskal Herria happens at the **taberna** (the bar). Knowing the basic drinks and foods in Basque will open doors for you right away: a group of friends, a visit to a new town, a *poteo*. Let's start with the vocabulary.

## Coffee and its variations

Coffee is probably what you'll order most often. It comes in several forms:

| Basque | English |
|---|---|
| *kafea* | coffee (generic) |
| *kafe hutsa* | black coffee (literally: "pure/empty coffee") |
| *kafesnea* | coffee with milk (from *kafe* + *esne*) |
| *kafe ebakia* | cortado coffee (*ebaki* = "cut") |
| *kafe deskafeinatua* | decaffeinated coffee |
| *infusioa* | herbal tea |
| *tea* | tea |

> *Kafesne* literally means "coffee-milk". Basque forms many compound words this way, without a hyphen and with the main word at the end.

## Other drinks

| Basque | English |
|---|---|
| *ura* | water |
| *zumoa* | juice |
| *esnea* | milk |
| *ardoa* | wine |
| *ardo gorria* | red wine |
| *ardo zuria* | white wine |
| *garagardoa* | beer |
| *zuritoa* | small draft beer (typical) |
| *sagardoa* | cider |
| *txakolina* | txakoli (Basque white wine) |
| *patxarana* | pacharán |

> Three curious words: **sagardo** (cider) = *sagar* (apple) + *ardo* (wine); **garagardo** (beer) = *garagar* (barley) + *ardo*; **txakolina** is a young, light white wine, a hallmark of the Basque Country.

## Basic bar foods

To go with your drink:

| Basque | English |
|---|---|
| *ogia* | bread |
| *gazta* | cheese |
| *urdaiazpikoa* | ham |
| *tortilla* | tortilla (potato omelette) |
| *patata frijituak* | french fries |
| *gozokia* | sweet (generic) |
| *txokolatea* | chocolate |

## The article `-a`

Almost all these words have **`-a`** at the end when they appear in a "neutral" form in a sentence: it's the article "the".

- *Ardoa gustatzen zait.* — I like the wine.
- *Ura nahi dut.* — I want water.

When you ask for "one", the `-a` disappears and **bat** ("one/a/an") is used:

- *Kafe **bat**, mesedez.* — A coffee, please.
- *Garagardo **bat**.* — A beer.

> You'll see this in detail in lesson 3. For now, memorize the forms with `-a` to recognize them on the bar menu.
