**NOR-NORI** verbs are those where something (NOR) "happens to someone" (NORI). The grammatical subject is **the thing**, not the person — exactly like *gustar* in Spanish: *me gusta el cine* (to me, the cinema is pleasing).

In A1 you already saw *gustatzen zait*. In A2 you'll expand this pattern to other very useful verbs.

## Present tense forms of the NOR-NORI auxiliary

For singular NOR:

| NORI | Form | English |
|---|---|---|
| niri | **zait** | me |
| zuri | **zaizu** | you (sg.) |
| hari | **zaio** | him/her |
| guri | **zaigu** | us |
| zuei | **zaizue** | you (pl.) |
| haiei | **zaie** | them |

For **plural** NOR:

| NORI | Plural form | English |
|---|---|---|
| niri | **zaizkit** | me (things) |
| zuri | **zaizkizu** | you (sg.) |
| hari | **zaizkio** | him/her |
| guri | **zaizkigu** | us |
| zuei | **zaizkizue** | you (pl.) |
| haiei | **zaizkie** | them |

## Past tense: *zitzaidan, zitzaion*

| NORI | Past sg. | Past pl. |
|---|---|---|
| niri | **zitzaidan** | **zitzaizkidan** |
| zuri | **zitzaizun** | **zitzaizkizun** |
| hari | **zitzaion** | **zitzaizkion** |
| guri | **zitzaigun** | **zitzaizkigun** |
| haiei | **zitzaien** | **zitzaizkien** |

## Verbs with this pattern

| Verb | Meaning | Example |
|---|---|---|
| *gustatu* | to like | *Filmak gustatzen zaizkit* (I like the movies) |
| *iruditu* | to seem | *Ondo iruditzen zait* (it seems fine to me) |
| *ahaztu* | to be forgotten | *Ahaztu zait izena* (I've forgotten the name) |
| *erori* | to fall | *Erori zaizu boligrafoa* (you've dropped the pen) |
| *gertatu* | to happen to | *Zer gertatu zaizu?* (what happened to you?) |
| *interesatu* | to interest | *Politika interesatzen zait* (I'm interested in politics) |
| *axola izan* | to matter | *Ez zait axola* (I don't care) |

## Structure: the subject is the thing

> **NORI + NOR + verb + NOR-NORI auxiliary**

| English | Basque | Notation |
|---|---|---|
| I like the cinema | *Niri zinea **gustatzen zait*** | NORI + NOR sing + zait |
| I like the movies | *Niri filmak **gustatzen zaizkit*** | NORI + NOR pl + zaizkit |
| It seems fine to you | *Zuri ondo **iruditzen zaizu*** | NORI + NOR + zaizu |
| He/She has dropped a book | *Hari liburu bat **erori zaio*** | NORI + NOR sing + zaio |
| I have forgotten the keys | *Niri giltzak **ahaztu zaizkit*** | NORI + NOR pl + zaizkit |

> **Tip:** the affected person is in the NORI case (-ari/-ei) and usually appears at the beginning. The thing is the grammatical subject, and the verb agrees with it.

## *Gustatzen zait* in the past tense — *gustatu zitzaidan*

To talk about likes in the past:

> *Filma asko **gustatu zitzaidan**.* — I liked the movie a lot.
>
> *Ez zitzaion gustatu opari hori.* — He/She didn't like that gift.

> Careful: *gustatu zitzaidan* (= I liked it, a completed action) vs *gustatzen zitzaidan* (= I used to like it, a habit).

## *Iruditu* (to seem) — personal opinion

One of the most useful formulas for A2:

| English | Basque |
|---|---|
| It seems fine to me | *Ondo iruditzen zait* |
| It seems interesting to me | *Interesgarria iruditzen zait* |
| What do you think? | *Zer iruditzen zaizu?* |
| It seems strange to me | *Bitxia iruditzen zait* |
| I think so | *Baietz iruditzen zait* |

## *Ahaztu zait* — I've forgotten

| English | Basque |
|---|---|
| I've forgotten | *Ahaztu zait* |
| I've forgotten the keys | *Giltzak ahaztu zaizkit* |
| He/She forgets everything | *Dena ahazten zaio* |
| Don't you forget! | *Ez zaizula ahaztu!* |

## Mini-dialogue

> *— Olatz, **zer iruditzen zaizu** Bilboko jatetxe berria?*
>
> *— Asko **gustatu zait**. Bereziki bigarren platerak: **interesgarriak iruditu zaizkit**.*
>
> *— Eta, jakin nahi nuen, **ahaztu zaizu** zer eskatu zenuen?*
>
> *— Bai, izenak ez **zaizkit gogoratzen**, baina arraina zen, ziur.*

## Common pitfalls

1. **The ergative subject does NOT exist here.** Don't say *\*Nik gustatzen dut* (it's a very common mistake): it's *Niri gustatzen zait*. The person is not the one performing the action.
2. **NOR agreement.** If the thing is plural, the auxiliary is plural: *zaizkit, zaizkio, zaizkigu...*
3. **Some regions use other variants** (*zaidak, zaidan* in *hitano*), but in standard Basque (*batua*), the standard forms are the ones in the table.

> **Cultural note:** Basque speakers often overuse (affectionately) *iruditzen zait* to express opinions with a certain caution: *interesgarria iruditzen zait*, *bitxia iruditzen zait*, *ez zait ondo iruditzen*. It's the Basque version of a nuanced "it seems to me that...".
