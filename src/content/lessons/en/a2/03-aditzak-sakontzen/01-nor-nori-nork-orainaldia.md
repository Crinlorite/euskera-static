The **NOR-NORI-NORK** is the **three-argument** verb: someone (NORK) does something (NOR) **to someone** (NORI). In English, this corresponds to constructions with two objects — *I give it to you*, *I told it to him*, *he showed it to me*.

It's the most complex verb in Basque, but at the A2 level, it's enough to master the **most common patterns**.

## The formula

> **partizipioa + d-i-NORI-NORK**

The auxiliary has three internal markers:

- **d-** at the beginning (present tense).
- **-i-** in the middle (it's the "root" of NOR-NORI-NORK).
- **-NORI-** as an infix (to whom).
- **-NORK** at the end (who does it).

## The most useful forms for A2

Table with singular NOR ("it"). Rows = to whom (NORI), columns = who does it (NORK):

| NORI ↓ / NORK → | nik (I) | zuk (you) | hark (he/she) | guk (we) | zuek (you, pl.) | haiek (they) |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| niri (to me) | — | **didazu** | **dit** | — | **didazue** | **didate** |
| zuri (to you) | **dizut** | — | **dizu** | **dizugu** | — | **dizute** |
| hari (to him/her) | **diot** | **diozu** | **dio** | **diogu** | **diozue** | **diote** |
| guri (to us) | — | **diguzu** | **digu** | — | **diguzue** | **digute** |
| zuei (to you, pl.) | **dizuet** | — | **dizue** | **dizuegu** | — | **dizuete** |
| haiei (to them) | **diet** | **diezu** | **die** | **diegu** | **diezue** | **diete** |

> **Don't memorize the whole table** at once. For A2, focus first on four cells: **dizut, diot, dit, dio**. With those four and their derivatives (digu, dizu, didate, diote), you'll be covered 80% of the time.

## Mnemonic pattern: the three letters

Read the forms following this internal order:

> **d** + **i** + (NORI) + (NORK)

| Form | Reading | Meaning |
|---|---|---|
| **d-i-zu-t** | "present + [i] + to you + I" | I ... it to you |
| **d-i-o-t** | "present + [i] + to him + I" | I ... it to him/her |
| **d-i-t** | "present + [i] + to me + (he)" | he ... it to me |
| **d-i-o** | "present + [i] + to him + (he)" | he ... it to him/her |
| **d-i-gu-te** | "present + [i] + to us + they" | they ... it to us |

> The NORI markers are **-da- (to me), -zu- (to you), -o- (to him), -gu- (to us), -zue- (to you all), -e- (to them)**.

## Agreement with the OBJECT (NOR)

If the object is **plural** ("them"), the form changes. For A2, it's useful to recognize the pair:

| Singular object | Plural object | English |
|---|---|---|
| dizut | **dizkizut** | it to you / them to you |
| diot | **dizkiot** | it to him/her / them to him/her |
| dit | **dizkit** | it to me / them to me |
| digute | **dizkigute** | it to us / them to us |

> *Eman dizkizut bi liburuak.* — I have given you the two books.

## And, of course, **NORK** takes -k

The subject of NOR-NORI-NORK is ergative (just like in NOR-NORK):

> *Lander**ek** Ainara**ri** opari bat eman **dio**.* — Lander has given a gift to Ainara.
>
> *Aitona**k** semea**ri** istorioa kontatu **dio**.* — The grandfather has told a story to the son.

## Typical NOR-NORI-NORK verbs

| Verb | Meaning | Example |
|---|---|---|
| *eman* | to give | *Liburua eman dit.* |
| *esan* | to say, to tell | *Egia esan didazu.* |
| *erakutsi* | to show, to teach | *Argazkia erakutsi diot.* |
| *oparitu* | to give as a gift | *Loreak oparitu dizkiote.* |
| *erosi* | to buy (for someone) | *Janaria erosi diot amari.* |
| *kontatu* | to tell, to narrate | *Ipuina kontatu digute.* |
| *bidali* | to send | *Mezua bidali dizut.* |
| *idatzi* | to write (to) | *Eskutitz bat idatzi diot.* |
| *eskaini* | to offer | *Lana eskaini diote.* |
| *gomendatu* | to recommend | *Liburua gomendatu didazu.* |

## Distinction: giving **to** someone vs. giving **something**

Compare the three types of "to give":

| Type | Structure | Example |
|---|---|---|
| NOR (no object): does not apply to *eman* | — | — |
| NOR-NORK (simple transitive) | *Opari bat eman dut.* | I have given a gift. |
| NOR-NORI-NORK (with a recipient) | *Opari bat eman diot Ainarari.* | I have given a gift to Ainara. |

> The recipient takes the **NORI case (-ari/-ei)**. You saw this in A1; here it is the verb's **infix**.

## Mini-dialogue

> *— Eskerrik asko, Olatz. Ohar polita **idatzi didazu**.*
>
> *— Tira, ezer ez. **Esan dizut** beti laguntzeko prest nagoela.*
>
> *— Anbotori ere **erakutsi diozu**?*
>
> *— Ez, oraindik ez **diot esan**. Bihar **kontatuko diot**.*

## Common pitfalls

1. **Don't confuse *dut* with *diot*.** *Dut* = NOR-NORK ("it"). *Diot* = NOR-NORI-NORK ("it to him/her"). If the sentence has a **recipient** (to someone), it's usually NOR-NORI-NORK.
2. **The recipient takes -ri/-ei**: *Anboto**ri** esan diot* (to Anboto), not *Anbotok* (that would be the subject).
3. **The subject still takes -k**: *Anboto**k** Garazi**ri** liburua eman **dio**.*

> **Pedagogical note:** the NOR-NORI-NORK table is famous for its density. The A2 strategy is to **first internalize the four or five most used cells** and recognize the others when reading. You will integrate them into your speech over time.
