This lesson completes comparisons with two more pieces:

1. **Excess and defect** (-egi, gehiegi, gutxiegi).
2. **Progressive comparative** (more and more / less and less: *gero eta...*).

Plus some useful modifiers to add nuance (*pixka bat, askoz, dezente*).

## The suffix -**egi** — "too"

To say something is **excessive**, you add **-egi** to the adjective (and then the final **-a** if it goes with a definite noun):

| Adjective | Excessive |
|---|---|
| *handia* (big) | *handi**egia*** (too big) |
| *txikia* (small) | *txiki**egia*** |
| *garestia* (expensive) | *garesti**egia*** |
| *beroa* (hot) | *bero**egia*** |
| *hotza* (cold) | *hotz**egia*** |
| *zaila* (difficult) | *zail**egia*** |
| *azkarra* (fast) | *azkarr**egia*** |

> *Alkandora hau **handiegia** da niretzat.* — This shirt is too big for me.
>
> *Kafea **beroegi** dago.* — The coffee is too hot.
>
> *Ikastaroa **zailegia** zen guretzat.* — The course was too difficult for us.

## *Gehiegi* and *gutxiegi*

As independent adverbs:

| English | Basque |
|---|---|
| Too much | *gehiegi* |
| Too little / not enough | *gutxiegi* |
| Too many people | *jende **gehiegi*** |
| Too little water | *ur **gutxiegi*** |

> *Gehiegi jan dut.* — I've eaten too much.
>
> *Gutxiegi lo egiten duzu.* — You sleep too little.
>
> *Hori **gehiegi** da niretzat.* — That's too much for me.

## The progressive comparative: *gero eta + -ago*

For "**more and more X**" or "**less and less X**", Basque uses a very expressive formula:

> **gero eta + adjective/adverb-ago(a)**

| English | Basque |
|---|---|
| More and more expensive | *Gero eta **garestiagoa*** |
| Colder and colder | *Gero eta **hotzagoa*** |
| Better and better | *Gero eta **hobea*** |
| Worse and worse | *Gero eta **okerragoa*** |
| More and more | *Gero eta **gehiago*** |
| Less and less | *Gero eta **gutxiago*** |
| Faster and faster | *Gero eta **azkarrago*** |
| Slower and slower | *Gero eta **polikiago*** |

> *Bilbo gero eta handiagoa da.* — Bilbao is getting bigger and bigger.
>
> *Eguraldia gero eta okerrago dago.* — The weather is getting worse and worse.
>
> *Gazteek gero eta gutxiago hitz egiten dute euskaraz.* — Young people speak Basque less and less.

> **Mnemonic tip:** *gero eta* literally means "later and" — the idea is "later, and later, and then more...". It's the Basque way of expressing progression.

## *Hain* — "so" (with or without negation)

| English | Basque |
|---|---|
| It's so big | *Hain handia da* |
| It's not so expensive | *Ez da **hain** garestia* |
| It's so beautiful! | *Hain polita da!* |

> For "not as... as..." you use *bezain* (previous lesson):
>
> *Ez da zuk uste duzun bezain ona.* — It's not as good as you think.

## Quantity modifiers

To add nuance to comparisons:

| English | Basque | Example |
|---|---|---|
| A little | *pixka bat* | *Pixka bat handiagoa* (a little bigger) |
| A bit | *zerbait / zertxobait* | *Zertxobait txikiagoa* (a bit smaller) |
| Quite a bit | *dezente* | *Dezente hobea* (quite a bit better) |
| Much | *askoz* | *Askoz hobea* (much better) |
| A whole lot | *askoz hobea / askoz garestiagoa* | |

> *Etxe hau zurea **baino askoz** handiagoa da.* — This house is much bigger than yours.
>
> *Pixka bat goizago etorriko naiz.* — I'll come a little earlier.

## *Hain... ezen* / *hainbeste...* (formal register)

Beyond a strict A2 level, it's useful to recognize:

- *Hain* + adjective + *non/ezen* — so... that: *Hain pozik zegoen, non kantatzen hasi zen* (he was so happy that he started to sing).
- *Hainbeste* — so much/many (emphatic quantity): *Hainbeste lan dago!*

## Mini-text

> *Garazi sukaldari ona da, baina kafea **beroegi** ipintzen du beti. Lehengo egunean **hain bero** zegoen, ezen ezin nuen edan! Eta hori ez da dena: pintxo bakoitzean olio **gehiegi** botatzen du. **Pixka bat gutxiago** beharko luke. Hala ere, hura **gero eta hobea** da: orain dela urtebete askoz **okerragoa** zen.*

## Mini-dialogue

> *— Olatz, etxe berri hori zer moduz?*
>
> *— Eder askoa, baina **garestiegia** zen guretzat. Eta logelak **txikiegiak** ziren.*
>
> *— Eta orain bilatzen ari zaretena nolakoa da?*
>
> *— **Gero eta zailagoa** da etxebizitza on bat aurkitzea. Prezioak **gero eta altuagoak** dira eta etxebizitza on guztiek dute lehia **gehiegi**.*

> **Cultural note:** the scarcity of affordable housing (*etxebizitza*) is a recurring topic in any A2-level conversation about current events: *gero eta garestiago, pisu eskuragarri gutxiegi, askoz okerrago lehen baino*. This lesson gives you the vocabulary to participate.
