This lesson integrates two very productive A2 blocks:

1. **Feelings** and moods (you know the basics from A1; here you'll expand on them).
2. **Discourse connectors** to argue and add nuance: *gainera, hala ere, beraz, alde batetik...*

With this, you'll close the unit and gain tools for more elaborate opinions.

## Feelings: the formula with *egon* + state

Most temporary feelings go with *egon* + adjective or adverb indicating a state:

| English | Basque |
|---|---|
| Happy | *Pozik nago* |
| Sad | *Triste nago* |
| Angry | *Haserre nago* |
| Nervous | *Urduri nago* |
| Calm | *Lasai nago* |
| Bored | *Aspertuta nago* |
| Tired | *Nekatuta nago* |
| Ashamed | *Lotsatuta nago* |
| In love | *Maitemindurik nago* |

> It's the same pattern from A1 (*pozik, triste, lasai*) expanded with *aspertuta, nekatuta, lotsatuta, maitemindurik*.

## Feelings with *izan*: *beldur naiz*

Some deep feelings go with *izan*:

| English | Basque |
|---|---|
| I'm afraid | *Beldur naiz* |
| You're right | *Arrazoia duzu* |
| I'm curious | *Jakin-mina dut* |
| I feel like it | *Gogoa dut* / *Gogoz nago* |
| I don't feel like it | *Gogorik ez dut* |

> *Beldur naiz autoz joateko.* — I'm afraid to go by car.

## Feelings with *NOR-NORI*: *lotsa ematen dit*

A very Basque formula: the feeling "gives you":

| English | Basque |
|---|---|
| I'm embarrassed | *Lotsa ematen dit* |
| It makes me sad | *Pena ematen dit* |
| It makes me angry | *Amorrua ematen dit* |
| It scares me | *Beldurra ematen dit* |

> *Hori esateak **lotsa ematen dit**.* — I'm embarrassed to say that.
>
> *Albiste horrek **pena handia ematen dit**.* — That news makes me very sad.

## *Jartzen naiz / jarri naiz*

To express a **change of state** ("to get nervous, happy, sad"):

| English | Basque |
|---|---|
| I get nervous | *Urduri **jartzen naiz*** |
| He/She got sad | *Triste **jarri zen*** |
| We got happy | *Pozik **jarri ginen*** |
| He/She gets angry over nothing | *Edozergatik haserretzen da* |

## Frequent psychological verbs

| Verb | Meaning |
|---|---|
| *gustatu* | to like |
| *maite izan* | to love |
| *gorrotatu* / *higuindu* | to hate |
| *beldurtu* | to get scared |
| *aspertu* | to get bored |
| *haserretu* | to get angry |
| *lasaitu* | to calm down |
| *poztu* | to become happy |
| *triste jarri* / *atsekabetu* | to become sad |
| *kezkatu* | to worry |

> *Asko **maite ditut** nire gurasoak.* — I love my parents very much.
>
> *Ez **kezkatu**, dena ondo aterako da.* — Don't worry, everything will be fine.

## Discourse connectors

To string together opinions and arguments:

### Addition

| English | Basque |
|---|---|
| Besides | *gainera* |
| And also | *eta baita ere* / *...ere* |
| Especially | *batez ere* |

> *Gainera, prezioa ere ona da.* — Besides, the price is also good.

### Cause / consequence

| English | Basque |
|---|---|
| Therefore | *beraz* |
| That's why | *horregatik* |
| For that reason | *arrazoi horregatik* |

> *Berandu da; **beraz**, joango naiz.* — It's late; therefore, I'm leaving.

### Contrast / opposition

| English | Basque |
|---|---|
| However | *hala ere* |
| But | *baina* |
| On the other hand | *bestetik* |
| Instead | *aldiz* / *berriz* |

> *Berandu da; **hala ere**, etorriko naiz.* — It's late; however, I'll come.

### Organizing discourse

| English | Basque |
|---|---|
| On the one hand… on the other | *Alde batetik... bestetik...* |
| First… then… | *Lehenengo... gero...* |
| Finally | *Azkenik* |
| In summary | *Laburbilduz* |

## A2 coordinated structures

These are constructions that appear in any A2 exam:

| English | Basque |
|---|---|
| Neither... nor... | *Ez... ez...* |
| Both... and... | *Bai... bai...* / *Hala... nola...* |
| Not only... but also... | *Ez bakarrik..., baita ere...* |
| ...as well (included) | *...ere bai* |

> *Ez Anboto, ez Olatz ez dira etorri.* — Neither Anboto nor Olatz have come.
>
> *Bai goizean, bai arratsaldean, lan egin behar dut.* — I have to work both in the morning and in the afternoon.

## Mini-text opinion

> *Niretzat **garrantzitsua** da gauean ondo lo egitea. **Alde batetik**, lasaitu egiten gara. **Bestetik**, hurrengo egunean indartsuago **gaude**. **Gainera**, osasunarentzat ezinbestekoa da: zazpi-zortzi ordu lo egiten ez **baditugu**, gaixotzeko probabilitate **handiagoa** dugu. **Hala ere**, ez da beti erraza izaten: askotan ezin dut lo hartu **kezkatuta nagoelako**. **Beraz**, lo egiten lagunduko didan errutina bat behar dut.*

## A2 Tip: the "opinion skeleton"

When you're asked to give an opinion in A2, follow this template:

> **1. Nire ustez** + topic + **-(e)la**.
>
> **2. Alde batetik**, + reason1 + **-lako**.
>
> **3. Bestetik**, + reason2.
>
> **4. Hala ere**, + nuance.
>
> **5. Beraz**, + conclusion.

With that, you can build a complete A2 paragraph.

> **Cultural note:** many official A2 texts (letter to the editor, forum, exam essay) ask for this argumentative structure. Practice it with everyday topics: sports, food, social media, work, town festivals.
