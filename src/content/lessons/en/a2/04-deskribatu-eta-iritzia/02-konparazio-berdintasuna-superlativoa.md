In this lesson, you'll complete the family of comparisons with two pieces:

1. **Equality** ("as... as", "as much as"): *bezain*, *adina*, *beste*.
2. **Superlative** ("the most..."): *-en + a*.

## Equality: *bezain*

For "as... as" with an adjective or adverb:

> **X bezain Y**

| English | Basque |
|---|---|
| As tall as you | *Zu **bezain** altua* |
| As beautiful as Donostia | *Donostia **bezain** polita* |
| As good as his father | *Aita **bezain** ona* |
| Not as fast as I thought | *Uste nuen **bezain** azkarra ez da* |

> Full sentence: *Lander **zu bezain altua** da.* — Lander is as tall as you.

## Equality of quantity: *adina / beste*

For "as much as" with a noun or quantity:

| English | Basque |
|---|---|
| As much as you | *Zuk **adina*** / *Zuk **beste*** |
| As much money as me | *Nik **adina** diru* |
| As many years as Anboto | *Anbotok **adina** urte* |

> *Zuk adina urte ditut.* — I am as old as you.
>
> *Garazik nik beste irakurtzen du.* — Garazi reads as much as I do.

> **Difference:** *bezain* goes with qualities (adjectives/adverbs). *Adina/beste* goes with quantities (countable or quantity nouns). People sometimes get them mixed up — the rule of thumb is: if the word that follows is an **adjective** → *bezain*; if it's a **noun or number** → *adina/beste*.

## Comparison of manner: *bezala / moduan*

Another form of equality, this time of **mode** or **manner**:

| English | Basque |
|---|---|
| Like you | *Zu **bezala*** / *Zure **moduan*** |
| I do it like you | *Zuk egin duzun **moduan** egiten dut* |
| A house like yours | *Zurea **bezalako** etxea* |
| He/She speaks like his/her mother | *Amaren **moduan** hitz egiten du* |

> Compare:
>
> - *Zu **bezain** altua* — as tall as you (quality)
> - *Zu **bezala** hitz egiten du* — speaks like you (manner)
> - *Zuk **adina** ikasten du* — studies as much as you (quantity)

## Superlative: *-en + a*

For "the most..." you add **-en** to the adjective or adverb + the article **-a**:

| Adjective | Superlative |
|---|---|
| *handia* (big) | *handi**ena*** (the biggest) |
| *txikia* (small) | *txiki**ena*** |
| *polita* (beautiful/pretty) | *polit**ena*** |
| *zaharra* (old) | *zaharr**ena*** |
| *azkarra* (fast) | *azkarr**ena*** |
| *garestia* (expensive) | *garesti**ena*** |
| *ona* (good) | **onena** / **hoberena** (irregular) |
| *txarra* (bad) | **txarrena** / **okerrena** |

> *Liburu hau **politena** da.* — This book is the most beautiful.
>
> *Donostia Euskal Herriko hiri **politena** da, askoren ustez.* — Donostia is the most beautiful city in Euskal Herria, according to many.

## Superlative + group: *-etan / -etako*

When you indicate **within which group**, you use the locative or genitive case:

| English | Basque |
|---|---|
| The tallest in the class | *Klaseko **altuena*** |
| The fastest in the world | *Munduko **azkarrena*** |
| The best of all | *Denetan **onena*** / *Guztietan **onena*** |
| The worst of days | *Egunetan **txarrena*** |

> *Klaseko ikasle **azkarrena** da.* — He/She is the fastest student in the class.
>
> *Euskal Herriko mendi **altuena** Hiru Errege Mahaia da.* — The highest mountain in Euskal Herria is Hiru Errege Mahaia (the Table of the Three Kings).

## *Gehien / gutxien* — the one who... the most / the one who... the least

Superlative equivalents for quantity or frequency:

| English | Basque |
|---|---|
| What I like the most | *Gehien gustatzen zaidana* |
| What I do the least | *Gutxien egiten dudana* |
| The one who studies the most | *Gehien ikasten duena* |

## Mini cultural comparison

Typical phrases:

- *Hiru Errege Mahaia Euskal Herriko mendi **altuena** da.* — Hiru Errege Mahaia (the Table of the Three Kings) is the highest mountain in Euskal Herria.
- *Korrika euskararen aldeko ekintza **handiena** da.* — Korrika is the biggest event in favor of the Basque language.
- *San Fermin festa famatuena da.* — San Fermín is the most famous festival.

## Memory trick

| Form | Example |
|---|---|
| Comparative (more X than…) | *X baino Y-**agoa*** |
| Equality (as X as…) | *Y bezain X* |
| Equality of quantity (as much as) | *Yk adina X* |
| Superlative (the most X) | *X-**ena*** |
| Superlative in a group | *Z-ko / Z-etan + X-**ena*** |

## Mini-dialogue

> *— Anboto, hau bai pintxoa! Sekula jan dudan **goxoena**.*
>
> *— Bai, **zure ustez** Bilboko **onenak** non daude?*
>
> *— Plaza Berrian. Han pintxoak **garestienak** dira, baina **goxoenak** ere bai. Donostiakoak ere ez dira **gutxiago**: ia **maila berekoak** dira.*

> **Cultural note:** in typical debates in *Euskal Herri*, *Donostiako pintxoak Bilbokoak baino hobeak dira* is a classic topic of friendly discussion. The reality: each area defends its own, and they are all delicious.
