Last unit of the level — the one for **tying up loose ends**. We'll start with the ultimate test of your past: **telling a whole life story**, yours or anyone else's. It's THE classic A2 exam exercise and, by the way, the best way to get to know someone.

## Life's Milestones

| English | Basque |
|---|---|
| To be born | *jaio* (jaio nintzen/zen) |
| To grow up | *hazi* |
| To go to school | *eskolara joan* |
| To study (a degree/course) | *ikasketak egin* |
| To start working | *lanean hasi* |
| To get married | *ezkondu* |
| To have children | *seme-alabak izan* |
| To move (house) | *bizitzera joan / etxez aldatu* |
| To retire | *erretiroa hartu* |
| To die | *hil* |

## Dates and Eras

> *1990**ean** jaio nintzen.* — I was born in 1990. (year + -ean)
>
> ***Gaztaroan** Iruñean bizi izan zen.* — In his/her youth, he/she lived in Pamplona.
>
> ***Garai hartan** ez zegoen internetik.* — In those days, there was no internet.
>
> ***Urte askoan** irakasle ibili zen.* — For many years, she worked as a teacher.

## A Well-Rounded Biography (Exam Model)

> *Elbira Zipitria **1906an jaio zen**, Zumaian, eta Donostian **hazi zen**. Gaztetan **irakasle-ikasketak egin zituen**. Gerra ostean, euskarazko eskolak **debekatuta zeuden**, baina Elbirak umeei euskaraz **irakasten zien** bere etxean, isilpean. **Urte askoan** aritu zen lan horretan, eta gaur egun euskal eskolaren **aitzindaritzat** hartzen dugu. **1982an hil zen**, baina bere lana gure ikastoletan **bizi da** oraindik.*

Notice the complete team: *jaio zen / hazi zen* (NOR), *egin zituen / irakasten zien* (NORK and NORI), *zeuden / zegoen* (descriptions)… **The entire A2 past tense in one paragraph.**

## Your Turn: The Skeleton

> *Ni **[urtea]n jaio nintzen**, **[herria]n**. **[Herria]n hazi nintzen** eta **[ikasketak] egin nituen**. **[Urtea]n [lana]n hasi nintzen**. Orain **[herria]n bizi naiz** eta **[zaletasuna] gustatzen zait**. Etorkizunean **[plana egingo dut]**.*

Past → present → future. Three tenses, one life: the perfect answer to "tell me about yourself".

## Asking About Someone Else's Life

> ***Non jaio zinen**?* — Where were you born?
>
> ***Non hazi zinen**?* — Where did you grow up?
>
> ***Zer ikasi zenuen**?* — What did you study?
>
> ***Noiz hasi zinen** lanean?* — When did you start working?
>
> ***Nolakoa zen** zure herria garai hartan?* — What was your town like in those days?
