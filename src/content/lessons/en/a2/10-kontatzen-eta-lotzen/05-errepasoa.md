Last lesson of A2. **There's no new grammar** — there's something better: proof that everything you've learned works TOGETHER. Exam format: read, listen to your inner voice in Basque, and answer. *Ea zer moduz!*

## The map of what's already yours

**From A1 you brought**: present tense, NOR-NORK, cases, time, shopping, likes.

**A2 has given you:**

| Block | The star |
|---|---|
| Health and imperative | *Hartu pilula hau!* |
| Past tense (x3) | *joan nintzen · joaten nintzen · joan ohi nintzen* |
| NOR-NORI-NORK | *eman dizkiot* |
| Comparisons | *handiagoa, handiena, handiegia* |
| Opinion and subordinate clauses | *uste dut … -la* |

## The final text (the whole level in one paragraph)

> *Iaz euskara **ikasten hasi nintzen**. Hasieran dena **zaila iruditzen zitzaidan**: aditzak, kasuak, hitz luzeak… Egunero apur bat **ikasi ohi nuen**, autobusean **nindoanean** edo afaldu **ondoren**. Orain, urtebete **geroago**, gauza asko **esan ditzaket**: nire bizitza **konta dezaket**, planak **egin ditzaket** eta lagunei mezuak euskaraz **bidaltzen dizkiet**. Ez dakit **noiz arte iritsiko naizen**, baina gauza bat **ziur dakit**: datorren urtean **B1 maila egingo dut**. Eta zuk? **Ea elkarrekin ikasten dugun**!*

Did you understand all of it? That, exactly, is what having an A2 level means.

## The honest self-assessment

You can say you've mastered A2 if you can do this without looking:

1. ✅ Talk about **what you did** last weekend (3 sentences in the past tense).
2. ✅ Talk about **what you'll do** next weekend (3 sentences in the future tense).
3. ✅ Ask for something in a store **and return it** the next day.
4. ✅ Leave a **phone message**: who you are, what you want, and ask for a call back.
5. ✅ Tell **your life story** in six sentences (past → present → plans).
6. ✅ Complain about the **weather** with some authority. ☔

## Zorionak!

*Hemen bukatzen da A2 maila. **Bidaia ederra izan da**: orain euskaraz **bizi zaitezke** — oinarrizko gauzetan, baina **benetan**. Hurrengo geltokia: **B1**. Han **ikasiko duzu** iritziak defendatzen, istorio luzeagoak kontatzen eta euskara **zure-zurea** egiten.*
