Your sentences already know WHAT happened; this lesson teaches them to say **WHEN** with clockwork precision: *when I arrived*, *before leaving*, *after eating*, *while you were sleeping*. Three pieces, and the combinations come together on their own.

## Piece 1: -NEAN (when)

It's built on the **-n** you learned yesterday in the zehar-galderak (see how it all fits together?):

| Direct | With "when" |
|---|---|
| *iristen naiz* | *iristen naiz**enean*** — when I arrive |
| *iritsi nintzen* | *iritsi nintzen**ean*** — when I arrived |
| *euria egiten du* | *euria egiten du**enean*** — when it rains |
| *txikia nintzen* | *txikia nintzen**ean*** — when I was little |

> *Etxera **iristen naizenean**, kafe bat hartzen dut.* — When I get home, I have a coffee.
>
> ***Txikia nintzenean**, herrian bizi ginen.* — When I was little, we lived in the village.
>
> *Deitu **duzunean**, bileran nengoen.* — When you called, I was in a meeting.

## Piece 2: AURRETIK / BAINO LEHEN (before)

**Participle + aurretik** (or *baino lehen*, which is equivalent):

> *Lanera joan **aurretik**, kirola egiten dut.* — Before going to work, I do sports.
>
> *Atera **baino lehen**, leihoak itxi.* — Before leaving, close the windows.

## Piece 3: ONDOREN / ETA GERO (after)

**Participle + ondoren** (or *eta gero*):

> *Bazkaldu **ondoren**, siesta txiki bat.* — After eating, a little siesta.
>
> *Lana bukatu **eta gero**, poteo bat?* — After finishing work, a *poteo*?

## The bonus: BITARTEAN (while)

> *Umeak **lo egiten duen bitartean**, aiton-amonek kafea hartzen dute.* — While the child sleeps, the grandparents have coffee.
>
> *Zu prestatzen zaren **bitartean**, ni autoa ateratzen ari naiz.* — While you get ready, I'm getting the car out.

## The routine, now stitched together

> *Goizean, jaiki **eta gero**, dutxatu egiten naiz. Gosaldu **aurretik**, korrika egiten dut ordu erdiz. Lanera **noanean**, podcast bat entzuten dut. Lana bukatzen **dudanean**, erosketak egiten ditut, eta afaldu **ondoren**, serie bat ikusten dugu. Ohera joan **baino lehen**, biharko arropa prestatzen dut. Aspergarria? Ez: **antolatua**.* 😄

## Dialogue

> *— **Noiz deituko dizut**? Lanean zaudenean ezin duzu hartu, ezta?*
>
> *— Ez. Deitu **lana bukatzen dudanean** — seiak **ondoren**.*
>
> *— Ados. Eta **afaldu aurretik** ala **ondoren** geratuko gara?*
>
> *— **Afaldu bitartean**! Pintxo batzuk hartuko ditugu. 😄*
