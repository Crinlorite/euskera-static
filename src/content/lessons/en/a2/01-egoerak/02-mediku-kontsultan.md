This lesson is a **complete doctor's visit simulation**. It combines vocabulary from the previous lesson with time expressions (how often, before/after) and common formulas. The doctor's visit is one of the most useful contexts for A2: it appears in any official exam.

## Asking for an appointment

> *Egun on. **Hitzordua eskatu nahi dut** familia-medikuarekin.*

Variations:

- *Hitzordua eman ahal didazu?* — Can you give me an appointment?
- *Larrialdietara joan behar dut.* — I have to go to the emergency room.
- *Bihar goizerako badago tarterik?* — Is there a slot for tomorrow morning?

## Frequency with -**ro**

To indicate "every X time", Basque uses the suffix **-ro** on the time noun:

| English | Basque |
|---|---|
| Every hour | *orduro* |
| Every two hours | *bi orduro* |
| Every eight hours | *zortzi orduro* |
| Every day | *egunero* |
| Every week | *astero* |
| Every month | *hilero* |
| Every year | *urtero* |

> It's the same *-ro* you already used in *egunero* (every day) in A1. Here you're just extending it to hours and other units.

## Before / after eating

The doctor's instructions often include these formulas:

- *Jan **baino lehen*** — before eating
- *Jan **ondoren*** / *Jan **eta gero*** — after eating
- *Goizean baraurik* — in the morning on an empty stomach
- *Lo egin baino lehen* — before sleeping

## "Better" / "worse"

To answer how you're feeling:

| English | Basque |
|---|---|
| I'm better | *Hobeto nago* |
| I'm worse | *Okerrago nago* |
| The same | *Berdin nago* |
| Much better | *Askoz hobeto* |

> **Note:** *hobe* (adjective) and *hobeto* (adverb) are irregular. There is no *\*onago* — the comparative of *ona* is *hobea*. We'll see this in detail in unit 4.

## Model dialogue

> *— Egun on. **Zer duzu**?*
>
> *— **Eztarriko min handia daukat**, eta uste dut **sukarra ere badudala**.*
>
> *— Noiztik?*
>
> *— Duela hiru egun hasi zen.*
>
> *— Ikusiko dut... Bai, eztarria gorri-gorri daukazu. **Hartu** pastilla hauek **jan ondoren**, **zortzi orduro**, hiru egunez. **Edan** ur asko, eta **hartu atseden**. Hobeto ez bazaude **astebete barru**, itzuli.*
>
> *— Eskerrik asko, mediku.*

## Key phrases to memorize

- *Hitzordua eskatu nahi dut.* — I want to ask for an appointment.
- *Min handia daukat hemen.* — It hurts a lot here.
- *Duela bi egun hasi zen.* — It started two days ago.
- *Errezeta emango dizut.* — I'm going to give you a prescription.
- *Errezetarik gabe saltzen al duzue?* — Do you sell it without a prescription?
- *Bajan nago.* — I'm on sick leave.

> **Cultural note:** Osakidetza (the Basque public health service) has its own bilingual terminology; many forms and signs appear in Basque first. Familiarizing yourself with words like *anbulatorioa*, *larrialdiak*, or *errezeta* will help you find your way around any center.
