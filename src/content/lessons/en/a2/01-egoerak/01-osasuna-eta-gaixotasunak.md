A2 opens the door to talking about how you feel physically: when you're sick, what hurts, and what the doctor has prescribed. The basic structure you already know from A1 (*mina daukat*) is expanded with specific vocabulary, healthcare professionals, and consultation dialogues.

## How to say "I'm sick"

In Basque, you have two common ways:

- **Gaixorik nago.** — I am sick. (a temporary state, with *egon*)
- **Ez nago ondo.** — I'm not well.
- **Min hartu dut.** — I've hurt myself.
- **Gaixotu egin naiz.** — I've gotten sick (intransitive verb, *naiz*).

> **Grammar note:** *gaixorik* takes the suffix -**rik** (state), just like *pozik* (happy), *nekatuta*, *triste*. Remember: state = *egon*.

## Symptoms — the universal formula X **daukat**

As in A1, symptoms are constructed with *eduki* (to have):

| English | Basque |
|---|---|
| I have a fever | *Sukarra **daukat*** |
| I have a cough | *Eztula **daukat*** |
| I have a cold | *Katarroa **daukat*** |
| I have the flu | *Gripea **daukat*** |
| I'm dizzy | *Zorabiatuta nago* |
| I'm nauseous | *Goragalea **daukat*** |

## Pain: *mina*

**Pain** is a noun in Basque (*mina*) and it's combined with the body part in **NONGO (-ko)**, which acts as a complement specifying "what kind of pain":

> **body part + -ko + mina + daukat**

| English | Basque |
|---|---|
| I have a headache | *Buruko mina daukat* |
| I have a sore throat | *Eztarriko mina daukat* |
| I have a stomach ache | *Sabeleko mina daukat* |
| I have a toothache | *Hortzeko mina daukat* |
| I have a backache | *Bizkarreko mina daukat* |
| My feet hurt | *Oinetako mina daukat* |

Another very common form, especially when something hurts "right now":

- *Buruak min ematen dit.* — My head hurts.

## At the doctor's office

| English | Basque |
|---|---|
| What's wrong? / What's the matter? | *Zer duzu?* / *Zer gertatzen zaizu?* |
| Since when? | *Noiztik?* |
| Does it hurt? | *Min ematen dizu?* |
| Take one pill every eight hours | *Hartu pilula bat zortzi orduro* |
| Rest | *Hartu atseden* |
| Come back in a week | *Itzuli astebete barru* |

| Place / Person | Basque |
|---|---|
| Health center | *anbulatorioa* |
| Hospital | *ospitalea* |
| Emergency room | *larrialdiak* |
| Family doctor / GP | *familia-medikua* |

## Mini-dialogue

> *— Egun on. **Zer duzu**?*
>
> *— **Sukarra daukat** atzotik, eta **buruko mina** ere bai.*
>
> *— Eztulik?*
>
> *— Bai, baina ez asko.*
>
> *— Ondo. **Hartu** pilula hauek **zortzi orduro**, eta **edan** ur asko. Hobeto ez bazaude, bihar etorri berriro.*

> **Cultural note:** in the Basque Country, the *"botikaria"* (pharmacist) is still, in many small towns, the first person to consult for minor issues: colds, insect bites, dizziness. The pharmacy acts as a filter before going to the family doctor.
