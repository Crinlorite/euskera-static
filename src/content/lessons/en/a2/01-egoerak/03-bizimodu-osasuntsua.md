Talking about **lifestyle** and the **environment** is a classic A2 block: it mixes new vocabulary with phrases of obligation and advice (*behar dugu*, *komeni da*, *garrantzitsua da*) that you've known since A1.

## Healthy habits

| English | Basque |
|---|---|
| To do sports | *kirola egin* |
| To walk / to stroll | *paseatu* / *ibili* |
| To run | *korrika egin* |
| To ride a bike | *bizikletaz ibili* |
| To eat fruit and vegetables | *fruta eta barazkiak jan* |
| To drink water | *ura edan* |
| To sleep well | *ondo lo egin* |
| To smoke | *erre* |
| To drink alcohol | *alkohola edan* |
| Not to overuse salt | *gatzaz ez abusatu* |

> Typical sentences:
>
> - *Kirola egiten dut astean hiru aldiz.* — I do sports three times a week.
> - *Erretzeari utzi diot.* — I have quit smoking.
> - *Ez dut alkoholik edaten astegunetan.* — I don't drink alcohol on weekdays.

## Frequency: *X aldiz*

To say "X times" you use the formula **number + aldiz**:

| English | Basque |
|---|---|
| Once a day | *Egunean behin* |
| Twice a week | *Astean bi aldiz* / *birritan* |
| Three times a month | *Hilean hiru aldiz* |
| Often | *Askotan* |
| Sometimes | *Batzuetan* |
| Seldom | *Gutxitan* |
| Never | *Inoiz ez* |

## Environment — *ingurumena*

| English | Basque |
|---|---|
| Environment | *ingurumena* |
| Nature | *natura* |
| Pollution | *kutsadura* |
| Garbage | *zaborra* |
| To recycle | *birziklatu* |
| To reduce | *gutxitu* / *murriztu* |
| To reuse | *berrerabili* |
| Renewable energy | *energia berriztagarria* |
| Climate change | *klima-aldaketa* |
| Global warming | *berotze globala* |

## The bins

| Usual color | Basque | What for |
|---|---|---|
| Blue | *edukiontzi urdina* | papera / paper |
| Yellow | *edukiontzi horia* | plastikoa / plastic |
| Green | *edukiontzi berdea* | beira / glass |
| Brown | *edukiontzi marroia* | organikoa / organic |
| Grey | *edukiontzi grisa* | hondakinak / other waste |

## Useful phrases for giving opinions

- *Garrantzitsua da birziklatzea.* — It's important to recycle.
- *Komeni da kirola egitea.* — It's a good idea to do sports.
- *Beharrezkoa da kutsadura gutxitzea.* — It's necessary to reduce pollution.
- *Ez dut uste plastikoa ona denik.* — I don't think plastic is good.

> These structures (*-tzea + adjective + da*) are the A2 formula for "it's important / a good idea / necessary to do X". We will work on them in depth in unit 4.

## Neighborhood life

| English | Basque |
|---|---|
| Neighborhood | *auzoa* |
| Neighbor | *bizilaguna* |
| Association | *elkartea* |
| Volunteering | *boluntariotza* |
| Neighborhood parties | *auzoko jaiak* |
| To participate | *parte hartu* |
| Meeting | *bilera* |

> *Gure auzoan jaiak antolatzeko bilera dago ostegunean. Ainarak parte hartzen du beti.*

## Sample mini-text

> *Lander auzo-elkarteko kidea da. Astean bi aldiz **bilerak** antolatzen ditu. Auzoko **jaiak** prestatzen dituzte, baita **boluntariotza-egitasmoak** ere: zaborrak biltzeko ekintzak, **birziklatzeari** buruzko hitzaldiak, eta abar. Berarentzat **garrantzitsua da auzoaren bizitza zaintzea**.*

> **Cultural note:** the *auzo-elkarteak* (neighborhood associations) are a fundamental part of social life in many towns in Euskal Herria. They organize parties, Basque workshops, mountain outings, and environmental campaigns. Participating is one of the best ways to practice the language in real use.
