From the landscape to its inhabitants: **animals** — those at home, on the farmstead, and in the forest. A rewarding vocabulary, full of compound words that tell stories (*basurdea* = "forest pig").

## At home

| English | Basque |
|---|---|
| The dog | *txakurra* |
| The cat | *katua* |
| The bird | *txoria* |
| The fish | *arraina* |

> *Gure **txakurrak** hiru urte ditu eta **katuak** hamaika. Nagusia, katua — noski.* — Our dog is three years old and the cat is eleven. The boss is the cat — of course.

## On the farmstead (baserria)

| English | Basque |
|---|---|
| The cow | *behia* |
| The sheep / the lamb | *ardia / arkumea* |
| The horse | *zaldia* |
| The pig | *txerria* |
| The hen / the rooster | *oiloa / oilarra* |
| The donkey | *astoa* |

> *Baserrian **behiak, ardiak eta oiloak** dituzte. Goizero **esnea eta arrautzak** hartzen dituzte.* — On the farmstead, they have cows, sheep, and hens. Every morning they get milk and eggs.

## In the forest and the sky

| English | Basque |
|---|---|
| The wild boar | *basurdea* |
| The fox | *azeria* |
| The wolf | *otsoa* |
| The bear | *hartza* |
| The deer | *oreina* |
| The vulture | *putrea* |
| The squirrel | *urtxintxa* |

> *Mendian **basurde bat ikusi genuen** — eta basurdea guri begira. Denok geldi-geldi!* — On the mountain, we saw a wild boar — and the wild boar was looking at us. Everybody freeze!
>
> *Pirinioetan **hartzak** daude berriro, gutxi baina badaude.* — There are bears in the Pyrenees again: few, but they're there.

## Talking about your animal

> ***Animaliarik baduzu**?* — Do you have any pets?
>
> *Bai, **txakur bat daukat**. Mendira eramaten dut **egunero**.* — Yes, I have a dog. I take it to the mountain daily.
>
> *Katuak **ez du ezer egiten**… eta hala ere, **dena da berea**.* — The cat does nothing… and yet, everything is his. 😄

> **Note:** animals populate the entire Basque language: *Otsoa aipatu eta otsoa atari* (speak of the wolf, and the wolf is at the door = speak of the devil), *azeria baino azkarragoa* (smarter than a fox)… And many surnames: Otxoa (wolf), Artzak (bears), Azeri… Animals have been here for centuries, in the names too.
