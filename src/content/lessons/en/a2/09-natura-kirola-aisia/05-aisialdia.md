## The tickets

| Spanish | Basque |
|---|---|
| The ticket | *sarrera* |
| To buy tickets | *sarrerak erosi* |
| Free | *doan* |
| Sold out | *agortuta* |
| The show / the screening | *emanaldia* |
| The queue / the line | *ilara* |

> *Kontzerturako **sarrerak erosi ditut** — azkenak! Ia **agortuta zeuden**.* — I've bought the tickets for the concert — the last ones! They were almost sold out.
>
> *Museoa **doan da** asteazken arratsaldetan.* — The museum is free on Wednesday afternoons.
>
> ***Zer ordutan hasten da** emanaldia?* — What time does the show start?

## Proposing the plan (all together)

> *— Aizu, **zer egingo dugu** larunbatean?*
>
> *— Bi plan ditut: goizean **azokara** joan — bertako gazta erosi nahi dut — eta arratsaldean **erakusketa berria** ikusi Artiumen.*
>
> *— **Ondo iruditzen zait** goizekoa… baina arratsalderako beste ideia bat: **kontzertua** dago gaztetxean, **doan**!*
>
> *— **Doan**? Ba erakusketa **datorren astean** ikusiko dugu. 😄 **Zer ordutan hasten da**?*
>
> *— Zortzietan. **Zazpietan geratuko gara**, zerbait afaltzeko.*

## Saying how it went (past + opinion)

> *Kontzertua **izugarria izan zen**: bi ordu eta erdi, jendea **dantzan** hasieratik. **Giro ederra**.* — The concert was amazing: two and a half hours, people dancing from the start. A lovely atmosphere.
>
> *Antzezlana **polita zen**, baina **luzeegia** — hiru ordu!* — The play was nice, but too long: three hours! (The *-egi* suffix, always reliable.)
>
> *Azokan **gazta, ogia eta sagardoa** erosi genituen. **Merezi izan zuen**.* — At the fair we bought cheese, bread and cider. It was worth it.

> **Note:** the Basque cultural scene has its own words: *gaztetxea* (the self-managed "social club" for cheap concerts), *bertso-saioa* (a session of *bertsolaris*), *herriko jaiak*… And the biggest *Azoka* of all: **Durangoko Azoka**, the Basque book and record fair in December — an annual pilgrimage for the Basque-speaking reader.
