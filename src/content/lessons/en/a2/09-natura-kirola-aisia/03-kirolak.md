Second stop in the unit: **sports** — the ones you do and the ones you watch. And in the Basque Country, that includes sports you won't see anywhere else in the world.

## All-time sports

| English | Basque |
|---|---|
| Sports | *kirola* |
| Football | *futbola* |
| Basketball | *saskibaloia* |
| Swimming | *igeriketa* |
| Cycling | *txirrindularitza* |
| Running | *korrika egin* |
| Hiking | *mendi-ibilia* |
| The gym | *gimnasioa* |

## The local ones

| Sport | What it is |
|---|---|
| *pilota* | Basque pelota, in the *frontoia* |
| *arrauna* | rowing — the *traineras* and the Flag of La Concha |
| *herri kirolak* | rural sport: *aizkora* (axe), *harri-jasotzea* (stone), *soka-tira* |

> *Herriko frontoian **pilota partida** dago larunbatean.* — On Saturday there's a pelota match at the town's fronton.
>
> *Irailean **estropadak** ikusten ditugu: gure herriko traineroa animatzera!* — In September we watch the regattas: let's go cheer for our town's trainera!

## Practice: the verbs

> *Kirola **egiten dut**: astean bitan **entrenatzen naiz** eta igandeetan **korrika egiten dut**.* — I do sports: I train twice a week and on Sundays I run.
>
> ***Igeri badakizu**?* — Do you know how to swim? (skills with *jakin*: *igeri dakit*, *dantzan dakit*…)
>
> *Gaztetan futbolean **jokatzen nuen**; orain **ikusi** bakarrik.* 😄 — When I was young I used to play football; now I just watch.

## The match

| English | Basque |
|---|---|
| The team | *taldea* |
| The match | *partida* |
| To win / to lose / to draw | *irabazi / galdu / berdindu* |
| The result | *emaitza* |
| To score a goal | *gola sartu* |
| The field | *zelaia* |

> *— Ikusi zenuen atzoko partida? **Zenbat** bukatu zen?*
>
> *— **2 eta 1 irabazi genuen**! Azken minutuan **gola sartu** zuten.*
>
> *— Aupa! Eta hurrengo astean **etxean jokatuko dute**?*
>
> *— Bai, igandean. **Zelaira joango gara**? Sarrera nire kontu!*

## Dialogue: signing up for something

> *— Kirol pixka bat egin nahi dut, baina gimnasioa **aspergarria** iruditzen zait.*
>
> *— **Zergatik ez zara** herriko igerilekura **apuntatzen**? Goizeko ordutegia oso ona da.*
>
> *— Ez dakit ba… **igeri badakit**, baina gaizki.*
>
> *— Horretarako dira ikastaroak! Ni **astean bitan** joaten naiz. **Elkarrekin joango gara**?*
>
> *— Beno, ados. Baina gero **hamaiketakoa** ere elkarrekin, e?* 😄
