New unit: **the outdoors**. We'll start with the scenery—mountains, forest, river, and sea—which in the Basque Country isn't just decoration: it's a Sunday plan, a secular religion, and an endless topic of conversation.

## The mountains

| English | Basque |
|---|---|
| The mountain | *mendia* |
| The summit / peak | *gailurra* |
| The slope / mountainside | *magala* |
| The forest | *basoa* |
| The tree | *zuhaitza* |
| The path / trail | *bidezidorra* |
| The rock / crag | *harkaitza* |
| The valley | *harana* |

> *Bidezidorretik gora, bi ordutan **gailurrera iritsiko gara**.* — Up the path, we'll reach the summit in two hours.
>
> *Basoan barrena **ibili ginen**, eta ez genuen inor ikusi. Bakea.* — We walked through the forest and didn't see anyone. Peace.

## Water

| English | Basque |
|---|---|
| The river | *ibaia* |
| The reservoir / dam | *urtegia* |
| The waterfall | *ur-jauzia* |
| The sea | *itsasoa* |
| The beach | *hondartza* |
| The waves | *olatuak* |
| The cliff | *labarra* |

> *Uda honetan ibaian **bainatuko gara**, hondartzan **jende gehiegi** dago eta.* — This summer we'll swim in the river, since there are too many people at the beach. (The *-egi* suffix and the causal *eta*, hard at work.)

## Describing the landscape

> *Hemendik **bista ikusgarria** dago: harana, basoa eta urrutian **itsasoa**.* — From here there's an impressive view: the valley, the forest, and in the distance, the sea.
>
> *Udazkenean basoa **kolore ederrekoa** da: horia, gorria, marroia.* — In autumn, the forest has beautiful colors.
>
> *Zuhaitz hori **oso zaharra da** — ehun urte baino **gehiago** ditu.* — That tree is very old: more than a hundred years. (Comparatives from unit 04.)

## Dialogue: the Sunday plan

> *— Igandean **mendira joango gara**? Anbotora, adibidez.*
>
> *— Anboto? Hori **gogorra** da niretzat! **Zerbait errazagoa**, mesedez…*
>
> *— Ados: Urkiolara **igoko gara**, bidezidor lasai batetik. **Gailurrean** hamaiketakoa.*
>
> *— Hamaiketakoarekin **konbentzitu nauzu**.* 😄

> **Note:** *mendizaletasuna* (love for the mountains) is pure identity: each town has its 'own' mountain and its summit with a mailbox. The word *hamaiketakoa*—the eleven o'clock meal—is the real reason many people climb. Don't say it too loud.
