What do you do when you're not doing anything? This lesson is about **hobbies** — and the piece of grammar that governs them: the verb turned into a noun with **-tzea** (*irakurtzea* = "the act of reading").

## The piece: -t(z)ea

From A1 you know *gustatzen zait* with things (*kafea gustatzen zait*). To like an **activity**, the verb becomes a noun:

> **irakurri → irakurtzea** · **abestu → abestea** · **bidaiatu → bidaiatzea** · **sukaldatu → sukaldatzea**

| Spanish | Basque |
|---|---|
| I like to read | *Irakurtzea gustatzen zait* |
| I love to cook | *Asko gustatzen zait sukaldatzea* |
| He/She likes to sing | *Abestea gustatzen zaio* |
| We like to travel | *Bidaiatzea gustatzen zaigu* |
| I don't like getting up early at all | *Ez zait batere gustatzen goiz jaikitzea* |

The whole NOR-NORI you already know (*zait, zaio, zaigu…*) is still in charge; only the "what" changes.

## The hobby catalog

| Spanish | Basque |
|---|---|
| Reading / writing | *irakurtzea / idaztea* |
| Listening to music | *musika entzutea* |
| Singing / dancing | *abestea / dantzatzea* |
| Cooking | *sukaldatzea* |
| Taking photos | *argazkiak ateratzea* |
| Painting / drawing | *margotzea / marraztea* |
| Traveling | *bidaiatzea* |
| Tending the garden | *baratzea zaintzea* |
| Video games | *bideo-jokoak* |
| Sewing / knitting | *jostea / puntua egitea* |

## Asking and showing off

> ***Zer egitea gustatzen zaizu** denbora librean?* — What do you like to do in your free time?
>
> ***Zein da zure zaletasunik gogokoena**?* — What is your favorite hobby?
>
> *Nire zaletasunik gogokoena **sukaldatzea** da: asteburuero **errezeta berri bat** probatzen dut.* — My favorite hobby is cooking: every weekend I try a new recipe.

## Three structures, three nuances (fine-tuning)

| Structure | Example | It means |
|---|---|---|
| **-tzea gustatzen zait** | *Margotzea gustatzen zait* | I like it (hobby) |
| **-tzen dakit** | *Margotzen dakit* | I know how to do it (skill) |
| **-tzen ari naiz** | *Margotzen ari naiz* | I'm doing it (now) |

> *Puntua egiten **ez dakit**, baina ikastea **gustatuko litzaidake**.* — I don't know how to knit, but I'd like to learn. (You'll master that elegant *gustatuko litzaidake* in B1 — we're leaving it here as an appetizer.)

## Dialogue

> *— Aizu, **zer egitea gustatzen zaizu** asteburuetan?*
>
> *— Niri **mendira joatea** eta **argazkiak ateratzea**. Batzuetan biak batera. 😄 Eta zuri?*
>
> *— **Sukaldatzea** eta gero… **jatea**, noski.*
>
> *— Hori ere zaletasuna da?*
>
> *— Nire etxean, **kirola**.* 😄
