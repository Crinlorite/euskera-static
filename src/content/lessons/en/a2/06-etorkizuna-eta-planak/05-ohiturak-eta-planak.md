Last lesson of the unit — and the end of a huge cycle: with the future tense in the bag, **you master the three tenses**. This lesson adds the finishing touch (*ohi*, the Basque equivalent of "to usually do") and has you move the same idea through the past, present-habit, and future. This is exactly what an A2 panel wants to hear.

## "Ohi" — the Basque 'to usually do'

> **participle + ohi + auxiliary**

| Spanish | Basque |
|---|---|
| I usually go to work by bike | *Lanera bizikletaz joan **ohi** naiz* |
| We usually eat at grandma's | *Amonaren etxean bazkaldu **ohi** dugu* |
| He/She usually comes late | *Berandu etorri **ohi** da* |
| I used to smoke (not anymore) | *Erre **ohi** nuen* |

Two notes:

1. **The position is fixed**: between the participle and the auxiliary. *Joan ohi naiz* ✓ — never *ohi joan naiz*.
2. With the **auxiliary in the past tense**, you get "used to": *joan ohi nintzen* (I used to go). It's a close cousin of the imperfect you already know (*joaten nintzen*) — *ohi* emphasizes the habit.

> **And without "ohi"?** The habitual present (*joaten naiz*) already expresses a habit. *Ohi* is an elegant reinforcement, common in written texts and exams. Use it when you want to sound polished: *Igandeetan hondartzara joan ohi gara.*

## Adverbs that accompany habits

| English | Basque |
|---|---|
| Normally | *normalean* |
| Most of the time | *gehienetan* |
| Almost always | *ia beti* |
| Sometimes | *batzuetan* |
| Almost never | *ia inoiz ez* |
| Every morning / every year | *goizero / urtero* |
| On Sundays | *igandeetan* |

## The complete map of tenses (your definitive cheat sheet)

| Idea | Basque | Tense |
|---|---|---|
| I go (habit) | *joaten naiz* / *joan ohi naiz* | habitual present |
| I am going | *joaten ari naiz* | present continuous |
| I have gone (today) | *joan naiz* | recent past |
| I went (one day) | *joan nintzen* | simple past |
| I used to go | *joaten nintzen* / *joan ohi nintzen* | habitual past |
| I will go | ***joango naiz*** | **future** |

Six boxes. One single verb. **All yours.**

## Mini-text: before, now, and tomorrow

> *Lehen, lanera autoz joan **ohi nintzen**: ordu erdi trafikoan, egunero. Orain bizikletaz **joaten naiz** — hamar minutu, eta gainera kirola **egiten dut**. Datorren urtean etxetik gertuago **biziko naiz**, eta orduan oinez **joango naiz**. Nire medikua pozik **egongo da**!* 😄

## Review dialogue (the three tenses in action)

> *— Zer egin **ohi duzu** igandeetan?*
>
> *— Normalean berandu jaiki eta familiarekin bazkaldu **ohi dut**. Baina datorren igandean ezberdina **izango da**: Behobia-Donostia lasterketa **korrituko dut**!*
>
> *— Ederki! Entrenatu **ohi zara**?*
>
> *— Bai, gaztetan asko korritzen **nuen**, gero utzi **egin nion**… eta aurten berriro **hasi naiz**. Bihar azken entrenamendua **egingo dut**.*
>
> *— Ba, zorte on! Helmugan **egongo naiz**, animatzen.* 💪
