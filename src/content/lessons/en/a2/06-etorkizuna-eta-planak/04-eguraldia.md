In a country where it can be sunny, rain, and be sunny again in the same morning, **talking about the weather is a national sport**. And it comes with a double prize: the *eguraldia* vocabulary is THE perfect ground to practice your newly learned future tense, because the forecast is always told in *geroaldia*.

## Basic vocabulary

| English | Basque |
|---|---|
| The weather | *eguraldia* |
| The sun | *eguzkia* |
| The rain | *euria* |
| The snow | *elurra* |
| The wind | *haizea* |
| The fog | *lainoa* |
| The clouds | *hodeiak* |
| The downpour | *zaparrada* |
| The temperature | *tenperatura* |
| The degrees | *graduak* |
| The forecast | *iragarpena* |

## The three key constructions

| Time | Construction | Example |
|---|---|---|
| **Right now** | phenomenon + ***ari du*** | *Euria **ari du**.* — It's raining. |
| **In general / today** | phenomenon + ***egiten du*** | *Hotz handia **egiten du**.* — It's very cold. |
| **Forecast (future)** | phenomenon + ***egingo du*** | *Bihar euria **egingo du**.* — It will rain tomorrow. |

Other phrases with their own verb:

> *Eguzkia **atera da** / **aterako da**.* — The sun has come out / will come out.
>
> *Haizea **dabil**.* — It's windy (the wind "walks").
>
> ***Hodeitsu / eguzkitsu dago**.* — It's cloudy / sunny.

> **Note:** in Basque, the weather "**makes**" things with *egin* and the auxiliary **du** (with no visible subject): *euria egingo du*, *bero handia egiten du*. It's just like the Spanish «hace calor» — except that rain is also "made".

## How to ask

> ***Zer eguraldi egiten du** zure herrian?* — What's the weather like in your town?
>
> ***Zer dio iragarpenak** biharko?* — What does the forecast say for tomorrow?
>
> ***Zenbat gradu daude** kanpoan?* — How many degrees is it outside?

## A real forecast (like on TV)

> *«Gaur, goizean **lainoa** izango dugu kostaldean, baina eguerdirako **eguzkia aterako da**. Arratsaldean **hodeiak** iritsiko dira mendebaldetik eta gauean **euria egingo du**, batez ere Bizkaian. Tenperaturak **behera egingo du**: 22 gradutik 16ra. Bihar, **haizea ibiliko da** eta **zaparradak** izango ditugu han-hemenka.»*

(Fog in the morning on the coast, sun at noon, clouds from the west, rain at night, a drop in temperature, wind tomorrow and scattered showers — the classic Atlantic menu. 😄)

## Dialogue: the plan depends on the sky

> *— Bihar mendira **joango gara**?*
>
> *— Ez dakit… **zer dio iragarpenak**?*
>
> *— Goizean **eguzkitsu**, baina arratsaldean **euria egingo du**.*
>
> *— Orduan goiz **aterako gara**. **Zaparrada** harrapatzen bagaitu, hurrengoan ez naiz zurekin inora joango!* 😄
