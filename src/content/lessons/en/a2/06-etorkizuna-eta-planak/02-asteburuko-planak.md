You already know how to build the future tense. Now it's time to **use it for what it's really for in daily life: making plans**. This lesson gives you the future time markers and structures to express intentions and decisions.

## Future markers

| English | Basque |
|---|---|
| Tomorrow | *bihar* |
| The day after tomorrow | *etzi* |
| This afternoon / tonight | *gaur arratsaldean / gaur gauean* |
| The weekend | *asteburuan* |
| Next week | *datorren astean* |
| Next month | *datorren hilean* |
| Next year | *datorren urtean* |
| Soon | *laster* |
| On vacation | *oporretan* |

> **Useful symmetry:** *atzo ↔ bihar* (yesterday ↔ tomorrow) and *herenegun ↔ etzi* (the day before yesterday ↔ the day after tomorrow). *Joan den astean* (last week, the one that went) ↔ *datorren astean* (the one that comes). Basque says it literally: **joan** = to go, **etorri (dator)** = to come.

## The key question

> ***Zer egingo duzu** asteburuan?* — What will you do on the weekend?

And its typical answers:

> *Larunbatean mendira **joango naiz**, eta igandean etxean **geratuko naiz**, lasai.* — On Saturday I'll go to the mountain and on Sunday I'll stay home, relaxing.
>
> *Oraindik ez dakit. Agian lagunekin **aterako naiz**.* — I don't know yet. Maybe I'll go out with friends.

## Intentions and decisions

| English | Basque | Nuance |
|---|---|---|
| I intend to travel | *Bidaiatzeko **asmoa dut*** | a plan in your head |
| I've decided to change jobs | *Lanez aldatzea **erabaki dut*** | a decision that has been made |
| I feel like going | *Joateko **gogoa daukat*** | a desire / a craving |
| I'm thinking of going | *Joateko **asmotan nabil*** | I'm toying with the idea |

The common structure: **verbo + -t(z)eko + sustantivo (asmoa/gogoa)**. With what you already know about *-tzeko* (purpose), this will come naturally.

## Examples in context

> *Datorren urtean euskara-azterketa bat egiteko **asmoa dut**.* — Next year I intend to take a Basque exam.
>
> *Aurten ez dugu oporrik hartuko; dirua aurreztuko dugu eta **datorren udan** Grezia ezagutuko dugu.* — This year we won't take a vacation; we'll save money and next summer we'll visit Greece.
>
> *Laster **jakingo dugu** emaitza.* — We'll know the result soon.

## Dialogue

> *— Aizu, zer **egingo duzu** etzi arratsaldean?*
>
> *— Etzi… ostirala da, ezta? Lanetik irten eta gimnasiora **joango naiz**. Zergatik?*
>
> *— Kontzertu bat dago Gasteizen. Sarrerak erosteko **asmoa dut**.*
>
> *— Ederra plana! Ni ere apuntatzen naiz. Gimnasioa **datorren astean** ere hor **egongo da**.* 😄

## Mini-text: my plans

> *Datorren hilean lan berria hasiko dut. Lehenengo astean jende berria ezagutuko dut eta dena ikasi beharko dut. Urduri nago, baina gogotsu: azkenean, benetan gustatzen zaidan zerbait **egingo dut**. Asteburuan lagunekin ospatuko dut — afari eder bat **prestatuko dugu** eta berandu arte **egongo gara** hizketan.*
