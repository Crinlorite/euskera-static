Knowing the future is all well and good, but social life calls for something more concrete: **inviting, making plans, meeting up, and responding** — saying yes, saying no, maybe another day. This lesson is pure conversational muscle.

## Inviting and Proposing

| English | Basque |
|---|---|
| Do you want to have dinner with me? | *Nirekin afaldu **nahi duzu**?* |
| Why don't we go to the movies? | ***Zergatik ez** goaz zinemara?* |
| Fancy a coffee? | *Kafe bat **hartuko dugu**?* |
| I'm inviting you | ***Gonbidatzen zaitut*** |
| It's on me (I'm paying) | ***Nire kontu*** |

> **Tip:** the most natural way to suggest something among friends is simply using the future tense as a question: *Kafe bat hartuko dugu?* (shall we have a coffee?), *Mendira joango gara larunbatean?* (shall we go to the mountain on Saturday?).

## Meeting Up: Where and When

| English | Basque |
|---|---|
| Shall we meet? | ***Geratuko gara?*** |
| Where shall we meet? | ***Non** geratuko gara?* |
| At what time? | ***Zer ordutan?*** |
| At eight in the square | *Zortzietan plazan* |
| I'll be waiting for you | *Zure **zain egongo naiz*** |

*Geratu* and *gelditu* are interchangeable here; you'll hear both depending on the region.

## Accepting

> ***Ondo iruditzen zait!*** — Sounds good to me!
>
> ***Primeran!*** — Great!
>
> ***Ados.*** — Agreed.
>
> *Bai, noski. **Han egongo naiz**.* — Yes, of course. I'll be there.

## Declining (with style)

> ***Ezin dut**, sentitzen dut. Lana daukat.* — I can't, I'm sorry. I have work.
>
> *Gaur ezin dut, baina **beste egun batean** bai?* — I can't today, but how about another day?
>
> *Eskerrik asko, baina **beste plan bat daukat**.* — Thanks, but I have other plans.

> **Cultural note:** in the Basque Country, the quintessential social plan is the ***poteo*** (*potea*): going from bar to bar having *potes* (small drinks) with your *cuadrilla* (group of friends). If someone asks you *«Poteoa egingo dugu?»*, you know how to accept: *Primeran!*

## Complete Dialogue

> *— Aizu, Maite! Larunbatean kontzertu bat dago Kafe Antzokian. **Etorri nahi duzu**?*
>
> *— Larunbatean… **ezin dut**, familia-bazkaria daukat. 😞*
>
> *— Lasai. Eta igandean? **Zergatik ez dugu** hamaiketako bat **egiten** Alde Zaharrean?*
>
> *— **Ondo iruditzen zait**! **Non geratuko gara**?*
>
> *— Plaza Berrian, **hamaiketan**. **Nire kontu** lehenengo potea!*
>
> *— **Primeran**! Han **egongo naiz**.*

## To Remember

Three pieces and you've got any conversation about plans sorted:

1. **Proposing**: *…nahi duzu?* / *Zergatik ez…?* / future tense as a question.
2. **Specifying**: *Non? Zer ordutan? — X-etan, Y-n.*
3. **Responding**: *Ondo iruditzen zait / Ezin dut, sentitzen dut.*
