Wrapping up the travel unit: the return. Because a trip doesn't end when you land — it ends **when you tell someone about it**. Here you'll put ALL the past tenses from unit 02 to work with the new vocabulary from this one.

## The questions they'll ask you

> ***Zer moduz** oporrak?* — How was your vacation?
>
> ***Nora joan zineten**?* — Where did you go?
>
> ***Zenbat egun** egon zineten?* — How many days were you there?
>
> ***Zer ikusi zenuten**?* — What did you see?
>
> ***Eguraldi ona** egin zuen?* — Was the weather good?

## The story: a structure that works

**When + where + what you did + an anecdote + the verdict.**

> *Iaz, uztailean, **Siziliara joan ginen** lagun batzuekin. Hamar egun **egon ginen**. Herri txiki batean **lo egin genuen**, itsaso ondoan. Egunero hondartzara **jaisten ginen** eta arratsaldeetan herriak **bisitatzen genituen**. Egun batean **galdu egin ginen** mendian — bi ordu bide okerretik! — baina paisaia **ikusgarria zen**. Bertako janaria **probatu genuen**: goxo-goxoa. **Oso ondo pasatu genuen**; benetan **merezi izan zuen**.*

Notice the mix of tenses, which you already master: *joan ginen* (specific point in time) + *jaisten ginen* (habit during the trip) + *zen* (description).

## Verdict phrases

| English | Basque |
|---|---|
| We had a great time | *Primeran pasatu genuen* |
| It was worth it | *Merezi izan zuen* |
| We'll be back for sure | *Itzuliko gara, seguru* |
| I didn't like it at all | *Ez zitzaidan batere gustatu* |
| Too many people | *Jende gehiegi* |
| Next time, with you | *Hurrengoan, zurekin* |

## The disaster anecdote (always a winner)

> *Itzuleran, **trena galdu genuen**. Hurrengoa bi ordu geroago **ateratzen zen**, eta geltokian afaldu **behar izan genuen**: makinako sandwich bat. Orain barre egiten dugu, baina momentuan… ez zen batere barregarria!* 😄

## Dialogue

> *— Kaixo! Bueltan zaudete? **Zer moduz Sizilian**?*
>
> *— Izugarri ondo! Argazki pila bat **atera genituen** — gero **erakutsiko dizkizut**.*
>
> *— **Eguraldi ona** izan zenuten?*
>
> *— Egun bat izan ezik, bikaina. Eta jendea, **oso jatorra**. Hurrengo urtean **itzuliko gara**.*
>
> *— Ba niri bertako **oroigarri** bat zor didazu!* 😄
