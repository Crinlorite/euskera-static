A trip is enjoyed twice: when you live it and **when you plan it**. This lesson is pure applied future tense: the plan, the list, and the suitcase — with everything you learned in unit 06 really at work.

## The plan's decisions

> ***Nora joango gara**?* — Where will we go?
>
> ***Noiz aterako gara** eta **noiz itzuliko gara**?* — When will we leave and when will we return?
>
> ***Non lo egingo dugu**?* — Where will we sleep?
>
> ***Zenbat gastatuko dugu**? Aurrekontua!* — How much will we spend? The budget!

## The order of the plan (connectors + future tense)

> ***Lehenengo** helmuga aukeratuko dugu, **gero** txartelak erosiko ditugu, **ondoren** hotela erreserbatuko dugu eta **azkenik** maleta prestatuko dut… bidaia bezperan, beti bezala.* 😄

## The suitcase

| Spanish | Basque |
|---|---|
| The suitcase / the backpack | *maleta / motxila* |
| The documents | *agiriak* |
| The passport / the ID card | *pasaportea / NANa* |
| The swimsuit | *bainujantzia* |
| The sunscreen | *eguzkitako krema* |
| The charger | *kargagailua* |
| The (travel) insurance | *(bidaia-)asegurua* |
| The umbrella | *aterkia* |

> *Bainujantzia eta eguzkitako krema **sartuko ditut**… eta aterkia ere bai. Euskal Herritik nator: **badaezpada**.* — I'll pack the swimsuit and the sunscreen... and the umbrella too. I come from Euskal Herria: **just in case**.

*Badaezpada* = just in case. A national survival word.

## Preparation verbs

> *Dena **prestatuko dut** asteazkenean.* — I'll prepare everything on Wednesday.
>
> *Ez dut ezer **ahaztuko**… espero!* — I won't forget anything... I hope!
>
> *Kargagailua **hartuko duzu**? Iaz **ahaztu zenuen**.* — Will you take the charger? You forgot it last year. (Bam: past + future in the same sentence, A2 level unlocked.)

## Dialogue: the long weekend plan

> *— Aizu, zubian lau egun izango ditugu. **Zer egingo dugu**?*
>
> *— **Zergatik ez gara** Zuberoara **joaten**? Landetxe bat **erreserbatuko dugu** eta mendian **ibiliko gara**.*
>
> *— **Ondo iruditzen zait**! Nik txartelak eta ibilbideak **begiratuko ditut** gaur gauean.*
>
> *— Eta nik maletak **prestatuko ditut**. Aterkia **sartuko dut**, badaezpada.*
>
> *— Noski. **Euria egingo du**, seguru.* 😄
