Second stop: **buying the ticket and not missing the train**. Here you'll recycle two tools from A1 — *zenbat balio du?* (prices) and telling time — and put them to work at the ticket office.

## At the ticket office

| English | Basque |
|---|---|
| The ticket | *txartela* |
| One-way | *joaneko txartela* |
| Round trip | *joan-etorria* |
| The ticket office / window | *leihatila* |
| The vending machine | *makina* |
| The schedule | *ordutegia* |
| The platform | *nasa* |

> *Kaixo, **joan-etorriko txartel bat** Bilbora, mesedez.* — Hello, a round-trip ticket to Bilbao, please.
>
> *— **Zenbat balio du**?* — How much does it cost?
>
> *— 8,50 euro. Trena **2. nasatik** aterako da, **hamarrak eta laurdenetan**.* — €8.50. The train will depart from platform 2, at a quarter past ten.

## Schedule questions

> ***Zer ordutan ateratzen da** hurrengo trena?* — What time does the next train leave?
>
> ***Noiz iritsiko gara** Gasteizera?* — When will we arrive in Vitoria?
>
> ***Zenbat denbora** behar da Iruñera?* — How long does it take to get to Pamplona?
>
> *Azken autobusa **gaueko hamaiketan** ateratzen da.* — The last bus leaves at eleven at night.

## Delays and punctuality

| English | Basque |
|---|---|
| It's running late | *berandu dabil* |
| It arrives on time | *garaiz iritsi da* |
| It has been canceled | *bertan behera geratu da* |
| There's a strike | *greba dago* |

> *Trena **hamar minutu berandu** dabil.* — The train is running ten minutes late.
>
> *Gaur **greba dago**; autobus gutxiago daude.* — There's a strike today; there are fewer buses.

## Dialogue: in a hurry at the station

> *— Aizu! **Zer ordutan ateratzen da** Donostiarako trena?*
>
> *— Hamikak **hogei gutxitan**… bi minutu barru!*
>
> *— Ai ama! **Zein nasatatik**?*
>
> *— Hirugarrenetik. Korrika eginda, **garaiz helduko zara**!*
>
> *— Eta bestela… **hurrengoa noiz** dago?*
>
> *— Ordu erdi barru. Lasai, kafe bat hartzeko denbora **izango duzu**.* 😄
