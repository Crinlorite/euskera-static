You already know how to get there; now it's time to **sleep**. From the hotel to the rural house (*landetxea* — very typical in the Basque Country), this lesson covers booking, check-in, and questions during your stay.

## Where to sleep

| English | Basque |
|---|---|
| The hotel | *hotela* |
| The guesthouse | *pentsioa* |
| The rural house | *landetxea* |
| The hostel | *aterpetxea* |
| The campsite | *kanpina* |

## The reservation

> *Kaixo, **erreserba bat egin nahi nuke** datorren asteburu**rako**.* — Hello, I'd like to make a reservation for next weekend.
>
> ***Gela bikoitz bat**, bi gau**etarako**, mesedez.* — A double room, for two nights, please.
>
> ***Gosaria barne** dago?* — Is breakfast included?
>
> ***Zenbat da gaua**?* — How much is it per night?

The grammar point of this lesson is the **destinative case -rako / -etarako** (for): *gau baterako* (for one night), *asteburu*rako* (for the weekend). You saw it in A1 with *norentzat* (for whom); this is its cousin for time and things.

## At the reception

> *— Arratsalde on! **Erreserba bat daukat**, Etxeberria izenean.*
>
> *— Bai, hemen dago: gela bikoitza, hiru gau. **Pasaportea edo NANa**, mesedez.*
>
> *— Hemen duzu. **Zer ordutan da gosaria**?*
>
> *— Zortzietatik hamarretara. **Hemen duzu giltza**: 214. gela, bigarren solairuan. **Igogailua** eskuinean dago.*
>
> *— Eskerrik asko! A, eta… **wifia badago**?*
>
> *— Bai, **doan**. Pasahitza giltzarekin batera daukazu.*

## Asking and requesting during your stay

| English | Basque |
|---|---|
| What time is check-out? | *Zer ordutan utzi behar da gela?* |
| Are there any rooms available? | *Gelarik badago libre?* |
| The room has a good view | *Gelak bista ederra du* |
| Can you give me a wake-up call at 7? | *Zazpietan esnatuko nauzu, mesedez?* |
| Everything is perfect, thank you | *Dena primeran, eskerrik asko* |

> **Note:** the *landetxeak* (rural guesthouses) are the jewel of Basque tourism: a farmhouse, a village breakfast, and silence. If you book one, you'll arrive saying *gela bikoitz bat nahi nuke* and leave saying *datorren urtean itzuliko gara*. 😄
