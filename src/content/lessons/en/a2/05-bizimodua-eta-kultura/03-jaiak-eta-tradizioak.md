This lesson gives you the tools to talk about **festivals and customs** and to **compare times and places** ("before... now", "both in X and in Y"). It's the essential vocabulary for any cultural conversation at the A2 level.

## Basque festivals: core vocabulary

| Date | Festival | Brief English |
|---|---|---|
| June 23 | *San Juan sua* | St. John's Bonfire |
| December 31 | *Urtezahar* | New Year's Eve |
| December 24 | *Olentzero* | Charcoal-maker character who brings gifts |
| February / March | *Inauteriak / Aratusteak* | Carnivals |
| February 5 | *Santa Ageda* | Traditional songs sung door-to-door |
| Every two years | *Korrika* | Popular race in favor of the Basque language |
| August | *Aste Nagusia* | Bilbao's Big Week |
| Local (each town) | *Herriko jaiak* | Town festivals |

## Characters and elements of Basque culture

| English | Basque |
|---|---|
| Bertsolari (verse improviser) | *bertsolaria* |
| Trikitilari (accordionist) | *trikitilaria* |
| Txistulari (traditional flutist) | *txistularia* |
| Dantzari (dancer) | *dantzaria* |
| Pelotari (pelota player) | *pilotaria* |
| Aizkolari (wood chopper) | *aizkolaria* |
| Harrijasotzaile (stone lifter) | *harrijasotzaileak* |

> **Cultural note:** the *kirol-jaiak* (rural sports) are typical of the festivals: stone lifting, wood chopping with an axe, *traineras* (rowing boats), *soka-tira* (tug-of-war). They are a hallmark of Basque identity and appear in any town's festival program.

## Comparing before and now: *lehen / orain*

Cultural comparison revolves around these two words:

| English | Basque |
|---|---|
| Before | *lehen* |
| Now | *orain* |
| In the old days / Formerly | *garai batean* / *aspaldian* |
| Nowadays | *gaur egun* |
| Before... now... | *Lehen... orain...* |
| Years ago | *Duela urte batzuk* |

> *Lehen baserrian **bizi ginen**, orain hirian **bizi gara**.* — Before we lived in the farmhouse, now we live in the city.
>
> *Garai batean **dena euskaraz egiten zen**; gaur egun, **erdaraz ere asko** egiten da.* — In the old days everything was done in Basque; nowadays, a lot is also done in Spanish.

## Comparing places: *bai... bai...*, *baita... ere*

| English | Basque |
|---|---|
| Both in X and in Y | *Bai X-(e)an, bai Y-(e)an* |
| Also in X | *X-(e)an ere bai* |
| Neither in X nor in Y | *Ez X-(e)an, ez Y-(e)an* |
| Not only X, but also Y | *Ez **bakarrik** X, **baita** Y **ere*** |

> *San Juan sua **bai** Donostian, **bai** Bilbon ospatzen da.* — The St. John's bonfire is celebrated both in Donostia and in Bilbao.
>
> *Korrika **bai** Hego Euskal Herrian, **bai** Iparraldean egiten da.* — *Korrika* takes place both in Hegoalde and in Iparralde.

## How to describe a festival

Useful structure:

> **Festival + when + where + what is done + why it's special**

> *San Juan sua **ekainaren 23an** ospatzen da. Herri askotan **plaza nagusian** egur pila bat egiten da, eta gauean **sua pizten dute**. Jendeak **suaren gainetik salto egiten du**, eta **dantzan eta kantuan** aritzen da gauerdi arte. Familia eta lagun arteko ospakizuna da, eta **udaren hasiera** ospatzen du.*

## Mini-text: comparison

> *Lehen, herriko **jaiak** ohikoak ziren udazkenean. Familia osoa elkartzen zen plaza nagusian, **bertsolariak** eta **trikitilariak** entzuten zituen, eta **dantzan** ari zen gauerdi arte. **Gaur egun**, jaiak antolatzea zailagoa da: jendea sakabanatuta dago, gazteek bestelako gustuak dituzte, eta **lehen baino** diru **gutxiago** dago. Hala ere, **tradizioa** mantentzen ahalegintzen dira: **bai herri txikietan, bai hiri handietan**, urtero programa **politak** prestatzen dituzte.*

## Dialogue: comparing customs

> *— Anboto, zer ospatzen da zure herrian?*
>
> *— Ba, klasikoak: **San Juan**, **herriko jaiak** abuztuan, **Olentzero** Eguberrian. **Korrika** ere bai bi urtero, oso garrantzitsua hori.*
>
> *— Eta Olentzero, oraindik etxez etxe ibiltzen da?*
>
> *— **Lehen ohitura zen** etxez etxe ibili eta umeentzako opariak banatzea. **Orain**, herri txikietan oraindik egiten da, baina hirietan ekitaldi nagusi bat plazan egin ohi da, **bertsolariekin eta musikarekin**.*

## Useful structures for comparing cultures

| English | Basque |
|---|---|
| Here, on the other hand... | *Hemen, aldiz, ...* |
| Just like in X | *X-(e)n bezala* |
| Unlike X | *X ez bezala* |
| It's similar to | *Antzekoa da* |
| It's very different | *Oso ezberdina da* |

> **Cultural note:** Basque festivals are one of the most common A2 topics in official exams and real conversations. Write a **description of your own favorite festival** in five sentences: when, where, what is done, what you like about it, and compare it to another one. It's a perfect integrative exercise.
