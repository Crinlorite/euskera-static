This integrative lesson reviews **four new declensions** that A2 adds to the A1 repertoire (NOR, NORK, NORI, NON, NOREN, NORA, NONDIK, NOREKIN). These are the ones that appear on any A2 exam and complete the Basque "rose".

## NORENTZAT — for whom (destinative)

> **-(r)entzat** on the definite noun

| Noun | Form | English |
|---|---|---|
| niretzat | *niretzat* | for me |
| zuretzat | *zuretzat* | for you |
| beretzat / harentzat | *beretzat* | for him/her |
| guretzat | *guretzat* | for us |
| zuentzat | *zuentzat* | for you (pl.) |
| haientzat | *haientzat* | for them |
| Lander | *Landerrentzat* | for Lander |
| the mother | *amarentzat* | for the mother |
| the friends | *lagunentzat* | for the friends |

> *Hau **zuretzat** da.* — This is for you.
>
> *Opari bat erosi dut **Garazirentzat**.* — I have bought a gift for Garazi.
>
> *Liburu hau **gazte ikasleentzat** da.* — This book is for the young students.

> **Variant:** there is also the form **-(r)endako**, synonymous in many dialects: *Garazirendako, niretzako/niretako*. The standard form is *-rentzat*.

## NORAINO — up to (limit)

> **-(r)aino** on the noun (follows the NORA rule: vowel → +raino, consonant → +eraino)

| English | Basque |
|---|---|
| Up to the mountain | *mendi**raino*** |
| Up to Bilbao | *Bilbo**raino*** |
| Up to here | *hona**ino*** |
| Up to where | *nora**ino*** |
| Up to the summit | *tontorr**eraino*** |

> *Korrika egin dugu **plazaraino**.* — We ran up to the square.
>
> *Bilbo**raino** etorri zen eta gero itzuli.* — He/She came up to Bilbao and then returned.

## NORANTZ — towards (direction)

> **-(r)antz** on the noun (same pattern as NORA)

| English | Basque |
|---|---|
| Towards the mountain | *mendi**rantz*** |
| Towards home | *etxe**rantz*** |
| Towards the south | *hego**rantz*** |
| Towards you | *zu**rantz*** |

> *Etxe**rantz** abiatu nintzen.* — I headed towards home.
>
> *Itsaso**rantz** dago etxea.* — The house faces towards the sea.

> **Difference with NORA (-ra):** *NORA* indicates a specific destination ("I'm going to Bilbao"); *NORANTZ* indicates an approximate direction ("I'm going towards Bilbao", with no guarantee of arriving).

## ZERGATIK / NORENGATIK — because of (motive)

To ask for the reason: **zergatik?** (why?). To answer with a noun: **-engatik**.

| Noun | Form | English |
|---|---|---|
| nigatik | *nigatik* | because of me (cause) |
| zugatik | *zugatik* | because of you |
| beragatik / harengatik | *beragatik* | because of him/her |
| Lander | *Landerrengatik* | because of Lander |
| the mother | *amarengatik* | because of the mother |
| love | *maitasunarengatik* | because of love |
| the rain | *euriagatik* | because of the rain |

> *Eskerrik asko **etorri zarelako, niregatik**!* — Thank you for coming, for me!
>
> *Berandu iritsi nintzen **trafikoarengatik**.* — I arrived late because of the traffic.
>
> *Zergatik egin duzu hori? — **Zugatik**.* — Why did you do that? — Because of you.

> **Note:** distinguish *-engatik* (cause: "because of") from *-rentzat* (recipient: "for"). In Spanish, "por" covers both, but in Basque they are different.

## NONGO / NOIZKO — of what place / time

You already saw *NON* (-an: in) in A1. **NONGO** and **NOIZKO** turn a place or a time into a **modifier** (attribute):

| English | Basque |
|---|---|
| The people **from** Bilbao | *Bilbo**ko** jendea* |
| The museum **in** Donosti | *Donostia**ko** museoa* |
| Today's newspaper | *Gaur**ko** egunkaria* |
| Tomorrow's exam | *Bihar**ko** azterketa* |
| The July festival | *Uztaileko jaia* |

> This is the **NONGO/NOIZKO** case (-ko / -tako after a consonant): 'from where' / 'from when' in an attributive sense.

| Form | What for |
|---|---|
| **-ko** singular | *Bilboko, etxeko, gaurko* |
| **-etako** plural | *etxe**etako**, mendi**etako**, urte**etako*** |

> *Atzo**ko** afari**a** ona zen.* — Yesterday's dinner was good.
>
> *Bilbo**ko** etxebizitzak garestiak dira.* — The housing in Bilbao is expensive.
>
> *Mendi**etako** loreak ederrak dira.* — The flowers from the mountains are beautiful.

| NORANTZ | -rantz | towards where? | *etxerantz* |
| NORENGATIK | -engatik | because of whom/what? | *euriagatik* |
| NONGO | -ko | from where (attribute)? | *Donostiako* |
| NOIZKO | -ko / -tako | from when? | *gaurko, atzoko* |

## Integrative mini-text

> *Joan den astean **Bilboraino** joan ginen autobusez. Helmuga: hiriko **museorako** sarrera bana erostea. **Bilboko jendea** oso jatorra izan zen. Buelta hartzeko, taxi bat hartu genuen **etxerantz**: euri-jasa hain handia zen, ezen autoa **euriagatik** hondatu zen. Azkenean, ordu bat **beranduago** iritsi ginen **Donostiako geltokira**. Hori bai, etxean opari bat zegoen zain: **niretzat**, ama maitearen partez.*

> **A2 Challenge:** write a sentence for each case using the noun *Garazi*. You'll see how the whole machinery of Basque works on a single person.

> **Pedagogical note:** these declensions are the "gateway to B1": if you master them, you can already build sentences rich in information (destination, motive, attribute, direction). In B1, the abstract uses are explored more deeply, and a few more are added (instrumental with causal value, extended partitive).
