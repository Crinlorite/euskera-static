A2 opens up the world of **work** for you, beyond "I'm a student / I'm a teacher". Now you get to talk about:

- Types of jobs and workdays.
- Job interviews.
- Resumes and experience.
- Making a **description of the job** you have or want.

| Spanish | Basque |
|---|---|
| Tell me something about yourself | *Konta iezadazu zerbait zuri buruz* |

## Relative clauses with -**(e)n**

To describe your job in more complex terms, it's useful to use the **relative clause**:

> **conjugated verb + -(e)n + noun**

| Spanish | Basque |
|---|---|
| The job that I have | *Daukadan lana* |
| The company where I work | *Lan egiten dudan enpresa* |
| The colleagues that I know | *Ezagutzen ditudan lankideak* |
| The salary that I earn | *Jasotzen dudan soldata* |
| The course that I took | *Egin nuen ikastaroa* |

> **How it's formed:** verb in present or past tense + suffix **-(e)n** + noun.
>
> *dut → dudan*, *du → duen*, *zen → zen*, *zuen → zuen* (when it already ends in -n, nothing extra is added).

> This is a key resource for A2 that appears in unit 5 with several uses: in the interview (*duzun esperientzia*) and in presentations (*ezagutzen dudan jendea*). It's explored more deeply in B1.

## Mini-dialogue: lan-elkarrizketa

> *— Egun on. Eseri, mesedez. **Konta iezadazu** zertan **lan egin duzun** orain arte.*
>
> *— Bai. Bost urtez **lan egin dut** Bilboko diseinu-enpresa batean. Han **daukadan zeregina** bezeroentzako proiektuak diseinatzea da.*
>
> *— Ondo. Eta zergatik nahi duzu **lanpostu hau**?*
>
> *— **Iruditzen zait** zuen enpresa **interesgarriagoa dela**, eta nire trebetasunak hobeto erabili **ahal izango ditudala**. **Gainera**, **lanaldi erdiko** modalitatea **eskaintzen duzuelako**, hori asko **interesatzen zait**.*

## Mini-text: job description

> *Lander **kazetari** lanetan dabil hamar urtetik hona. Bilboko egunkari batean **lan egiten du**, **lanaldi osoan**. Goizeko zortzietan hasten da eta bostetan amaitzen du. Berarentzat **garrantzitsuena** albisteak ondo **kontatzea** da. **Soldata** ez da oso handia, baina **gustatzen zaion lana** dauka, eta **lankide jatorrak** ditu.*

> **Cultural note:** in the Basque Country, many public sector job offers (Osakidetza, Basque Government, city councils) award points for Basque language proficiency through a system of **"profiles"** (PL1, PL2, PL3, PL4). Learning Basque isn't just a cultural pursuit: for many public sector jobs, it gives you a competitive edge.
