This lesson extends the past tense to **NOR-NORK** verbs (transitive: I it, you it, he/she it). It's the foundation for narrating all actions that have an object: *I ate, I saw, I did, I said, I bought, I called...*

## The formula

> **partizipioa + nuen / zenuen / zuen / genuen / zenuten / zuten**

| Pronoun | Past tense of *dut* | English |
|---|---|---|
| nik | **nuen** | I it |
| zuk | **zenuen** | you it |
| hark | **zuen** | he/she it |
| guk | **genuen** | we it |
| zuek | **zenuten** | you (pl.) it |
| haiek | **zuten** | they it |

> The forms **always** end in **-n**, and the first letter is the person marker: **n-** (I), **z-** (you/you pl./they), **g-** (we).

## Plural object — *nituen*

If the object is **plural** (the books, the tickets, the beers), the auxiliary changes to the **-itu-** series:

| Pronoun | Plural auxiliary | English |
|---|---|---|
| nik | **nituen** | I them |
| zuk | **zenituen** | you them |
| hark | **zituen** | he/she them |
| guk | **genituen** | we them |
| zuek | **zenituzten** | you (pl.) them |
| haiek | **zituzten** | they them |

## Examples

| English | Basque |
|---|---|
| I ate pintxos | *Pintxoak jan **nituen*** |
| I saw a movie | *Film bat ikusi **nuen*** |
| They made dinner | *Afaria egin **zuten*** |
| What did he/she say? | *Zer esan **zuen**?* |
| We bought bread | *Ogia erosi **genuen*** |
| He/she sold the two motorcycles | *Bi motoak saldu **zituen*** |

## Synthetic verbs: *eduki* and *jakin*

Two very common verbs keep their **synthetic** form in the past, just like in the present (*daukat, dakit*):

### *Eduki* (to have, possession)

| Pronoun | Form | English |
|---|---|---|
| ni | **neukan** | I had |
| zu | **zeneukan** | you had |
| hura | **zeukan** | he/she had |
| gu | **geneukan** | we had |
| zuek | **zeneukaten** | you (pl.) had |
| haiek | **zeukaten** | they had |

### *Jakin* (to know)

| Pronoun | Form | English |
|---|---|---|
| ni | **nekien** | I knew |
| zu | **zenekien** | you knew |
| hura | **zekien** | he/she knew |
| gu | **genekien** | we knew |
| zuek | **zenekiten** | you (pl.) knew |
| haiek | **zekiten** | they knew |

> *Anbotok bizikleta gorri bat **zeukan** umetan.* — Anboto had a red bike as a child.
>
> *Ez **zekien** non bizi ginen.* — He/she didn't know where we lived.

## Two key details of NOR-NORK in the past

**1. The -k marker on the subject.** Just like in the present, the subject performing the action takes the ergative case:

> *Garazi**k** afaria egin **zuen**.* — Garazi made dinner.
>
> *Lander**rek** sarrerak erosi **zituen**.* — Lander bought the tickets.

**2. Agreement with the object.** If the object is singular → *nuen / zuen*; if it's plural → *nituen / zituen*. It's the same rule as in the present tense.

## Negation

> *Atzo ez nuen filmik ikusi.* — I didn't see any movie yesterday.
>
> *Ainarak ez zuen ezer esan.* — Ainara didn't say anything.
>
> *Ez genituen sarrerak erosi.* — We didn't buy the tickets.

> When the object is negated, it often appears in the partitive case: *filmik, ezer, txikirik*. It's the same *-rik* you saw in A1.

## Mini-narrative

> *Aurreko larunbatean Olatz eta Lander itsasora **joan ziren** (NOR). Hondartzan egun osoa **eman zuten**: igeri **egin zuten**, eguzkitan etzanda **egon ziren** eta liburu bat **irakurri zuten**. Iluntzean afari bat **prestatu zuten** eta lagunekin **afaldu zuten**.*

Notice how NOR forms (*joan ziren, egon ziren*) and NOR-NORK forms (*eman zuten, irakurri zuten, prestatu zuten*) are mixed. The two lessons combine to build a narrative.

> **Note:** in the next lesson, you'll see the **synthetic verbs in the past** (*nindoan, nentorren, nenbilen*) which complete the NOR past tense.
