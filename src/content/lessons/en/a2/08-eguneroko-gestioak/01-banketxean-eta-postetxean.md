New unit: **practical life**. Nobody travels every day, but everyone ends up at the bank or the post office. Let's start there — and you'll see how much A1 you'll recycle: numbers, prices, *nahi dut*, *behar dut*…

## At the bank (banketxea)

| English | Basque |
|---|---|
| The account | *kontua* |
| To open an account | *kontua ireki / zabaldu* |
| To withdraw money | *dirua atera* |
| To deposit money | *dirua sartu* |
| The transfer | *transferentzia* |
| The card | *txartela* |
| The ATM | *kutxazaina* |
| The commission | *komisioa* |

> *Egun on, **kontu bat ireki nahi nuke**.* — Good morning, I'd like to open an account.
>
> ***Transferentzia bat egin behar dut** Alemaniara. **Komisiorik badago**?* — I need to make a transfer to Germany. Is there a commission?
>
> *Kutxazaina **hondatuta dago**; barruan atera dezakezu dirua.* — The ATM is out of order; you can withdraw money inside.

*Hondatuta dago* = it's broken / out of order. A useful word for ATMs, ticket machines, and umbrellas in Bilbao.

## At the post office (postetxea)

| English | Basque |
|---|---|
| The letter | *gutuna* |
| The package | *paketea* |
| The stamp | *zigilua* |
| To send / to receive | *bidali / jaso* |
| Registered | *ziurtagiriarekin* |
| The mailbox | *postontzia* |

> *Pakete hau **bidali nahi dut** Iruñera. **Zenbat balio du**?* — I want to send this package to Pamplona. How much does it cost?
>
> *— **Arrunta ala ziurtagiriarekin**?* — Standard or registered?
>
> *— Ziurtagiriarekin, mesedez. Garrantzitsua da.*
>
> *— Ederki. **Hemen sinatu**, mesedez.* — Perfect. Sign here, please.

## Package notification (the dreaded little slip)

> *«Zure paketea **postetxean jaso dezakezu**, astelehenetik ostiralera, 8:30etik 14:30era. **NANa ekarri**, mesedez.»* — You can pick up your package at the post office, from Monday to Friday, 8:30 AM to 2:30 PM. Please bring your ID.

Yes: morning hours. Some things are the same in every language. 😄

## Full dialogue

> *— Egun on! Pakete bat **jasotzeko** etorri naiz. Hau da abisua.*
>
> *— Egun on. **NANa**, mesedez… Bai, hemen dago. **Hemen sinatu**.*
>
> *— Bide batez, **hiru zigilu** ere nahi ditut, gutun batzuk bidaltzeko.*
>
> *— Noski. **Guztira, 5,20 euro**. Txartelaz ala eskudirutan?*
>
> *— **Txartelaz**, mesedez.*
