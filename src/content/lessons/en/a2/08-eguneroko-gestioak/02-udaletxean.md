The final boss of adult life: **paperwork**. Breathe: with ten well-chosen words (*agiria, hitzordua, bete, epea…*) you'll survive any counter — in Basque, no less, with a free smile from the civil servant.

## The vocabulary of paperwork

| Spanish | Basque |
|---|---|
| The city hall | *udaletxea* |
| The municipal register / to register | *errolda / erroldatu* |
| The certificate of municipal registration | *errolda-agiria* |
| The document / certificate | *agiria* |
| The ID card | *NANa* |
| The driver's license | *gidabaimena* |
| The application | *eskabidea* |
| To fill out | *bete* |
| The appointment / turn | *hitzordua / txanda* |
| The deadline | *epea* |
| The counter | *leihatila* |

## Asking for an appointment (the modern ritual)

> ***Hitzordua eskatu behar da** aurretik, webgunean edo telefonoz.* — You have to make an appointment beforehand, on the website or by phone.
>
> *Hitzordua daukat **gaurko, hamaiketan**, erroldarako.* — I have an appointment for today, at eleven, for the municipal registration.

## At the counter

> *— Egun on. **Errolda-agiri bat behar dut**, banketxerako.*
>
> *— **NANa**, mesedez… Momentu bat… Tori, hemen duzu. **Doan da**.*
>
> *— Eskerrik asko! Eta beste kontu bat: etxez aldatu naiz. **Erroldatzeko**, zer egin behar dut?*
>
> *— **Eskabide hau bete** eta etxebizitzaren kontratua ekarri. **Fotokopiarik ez** da behar; guk egiten dugu.*
>
> *— Bikain. **Eperik badago**?*
>
> *— Ez, lasai, edonoiz etor zaitezke. Baina **hitzordua eskatu** lehenago, e!*

## Three survival phrases

> ***Zer agiri ekarri behar ditut**?* — What documents do I have to bring?
>
> ***Noiz arte** daukat epea?* — What's the deadline?
>
> ***Nork sinatu behar du**?* — Who has to sign?

> **Note:** *hitzordua* is literally "word hour" (*hitza* + *ordua*) — a doctor's appointment, a city hall appointment, or a romantic date: it's the same word. The context (and the face you make) does the rest. 😄
