Speaking on the phone in another language can be intimidating — you can't see the other person's face, there are no gestures. That's why this lesson is **purely formulaic**: set phrases that always work, from a formal call to a WhatsApp voice note.

## Answering and asking

| Spanish | Basque |
|---|---|
| Yes? Hello? | *Bai, esan?* |
| Who is it? | *Nor da?* |
| Is Ane there? | *Ane dago?* |
| It's Mikel | *Mikel naiz* |
| One moment, I'll put them on | *Une batez, orain jarriko da* |
| They're not here right now | *Orain ez dago* |

## When they can't come to the phone

> *— Kaixo, **Ane dago**?*
>
> *— Ez, **orain ez dago**. Zerbait **esan behar diot**?*
>
> *— Bai, mesedez: bilera **bihar dela**, ez gaur. Edo hobeto: **deituko didala**, libre dagoenean.*
>
> *— Ederki, **esango diot**. Nor zarela esango diot?*
>
> *— **Mikel naiz**, lanekoa. Eskerrik asko!*

Notice: *esan behar diot* (I have to tell them), *esango diot* (I will tell them), *deituko didala* (that they call me) — the NOR-NORI-NORK from unit 03 rules the phone. To call is *deitu* + NORI: *deitu diot* (I have called them), *deituko dizut* (I will call you).

## Technical problems (the classics)

| Spanish | Basque |
|---|---|
| The line is busy | *Komunikatzen ari da* |
| There's no signal | *Estaldurarik ez dago* |
| I've run out of battery | *Bateria gabe geratu naiz* |
| The connection is bad | *Gaizki entzuten da* |
| The call dropped | *Deia moztu da* |
| Wrong number | *Okerreko zenbakia* |

> *Barkatu, **gaizki entzuten da**. Igogailuan nago — **gero deituko dizut**!* — Sorry, the connection is bad. I'm in an elevator — I'll call you later!

## Messages (gaur egungo bizitza)

> ***Mezu bat bidali dizut** WhatsAppez.* — I've sent you a message on WhatsApp.
>
> ***Audioa entzun duzu**?* — Have you listened to the voice note?
>
> *Deitu beharrean, **idatzi**, mesedez: bileran nago.* — Instead of calling, please write: I'm in a meeting.

## The answering machine / voicemail

> *«Kaixo! Orain ezin dut telefonoa hartu. **Utzi mezua** seinalearen ondoren, eta **lehenbailehen deituko dizut**. Eskerrik asko!»* — Hi! I can't take the call right now. Please leave a message after the tone, and I'll call you back as soon as possible. Thanks!

*Lehenbailehen* = as soon as possible. An elegant word that raises your perceived level by two points. 😄
